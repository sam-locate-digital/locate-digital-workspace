## Critical issues (must fix before submission)

### 1. Sole applicant vs joint existing loan / likely joint ownership
- The existing Westpac loan (05) is held by **Michael A Rossi & Elena M Rossi** (joint), secured against 40 George Street.
- The new application (07) is a **sole application in Michael's name only**.
- You cannot cleanly refinance a jointly-held mortgage into a single borrower's name without addressing the co-owner. Either Elena is a co-applicant, or there is a title transfer / spousal transfer of her share (which changes the deal type, may trigger stamp duty, and requires her consent and legal advice).
- **Action:** Confirm who is on the Certificate of Title. If Elena is a joint owner, either add her as co-applicant or structure a transfer of ownership. Obtain her consent either way. This decision drives servicing, VOI, and document requirements for the rest of the file.

### 2. Declared living expenses materially understated vs bank evidence
- Fact-find (07) declares **living expenses of $2,000/month**.
- The NAB transaction extract (06) evidences substantially higher spend. Excluding the two loan repayments (home loan $2,410 and Latitude $680), living commitments are approximately:
  - Groceries $1,940 + School fees $1,150 + Dining/misc $910 + Fuel $520 + Private health $340 = **~$4,860/month**.
- Declared expenses are roughly **2.4x lower** than what one month of statements shows, and well below any reasonable HEM benchmark for a Fitzroy family with school-age children. Under responsible-lending obligations this gap will be picked up by the lender and must be corrected.
- **Action:** Re-do the living-expenses section with the applicant against actual statements and HEM categories. Expect the revised figure to change borrowing capacity — re-check servicing before submitting.
- Note: the transaction doc's own stated total ("~$4,300 excl. business costs") does not reconcile with its line items (which sum to ~$7,950 including debts, or ~$4,860 excluding debts). Clarify and use verified figures.
