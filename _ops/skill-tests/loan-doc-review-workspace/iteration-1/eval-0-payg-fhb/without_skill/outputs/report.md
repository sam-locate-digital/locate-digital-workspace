## 1. Critical issues (must fix before lodging)

### 1.1 Undeclared credit card (debts declared "NIL")
The fact-find records **existing debts: NIL**, but the CommBank savings statement shows a recurring **"CBA CREDITCARD PYMT" of $400** on 9 Jun, 23 Jun and 23 Jul. Sarah clearly holds a credit card.

- This is an **undisclosed liability**. It must be declared on the application.
- The lender assesses serviceability on the **credit limit**, not the balance or repayment — even a $0 balance card reduces borrowing capacity.
- Lodging with "NIL debts" against a credit file that will show the card is a data-accuracy / responsible-lending problem and risks the application being withdrawn or declined.
- **Action:** Obtain the current credit card statement (limit, balance, lender) and re-run serviceability. Confirm whether it stays open or is to be closed/reduced at settlement.

### 1.2 Funds to complete — shortfall
The numbers don't currently add up:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | $720,000 |
| Less loan | $655,000 |
| **Deposit/equity required from Sarah** | **$65,000** |

Even before stamp duty, her savings ($46,520) do not cover the $65,000 equity contribution. There is a **material shortfall**, and the contract already requires a **10% deposit of $72,000** which the savings balance cannot fund.

- **Action:** Establish exactly where the full deposit and completion funds are coming from. Is there a gift, a First Home Buyer scheme (e.g. Home Guarantee / low-deposit scheme), a second account, or is the loan amount/LVR wrong? This must reconcile before lodging.

### 1.3 Large unexplained deposit — genuine savings problem
The statement shows **"TRANSFER FROM EXTERNAL $35,000" on 20 Jul 2026**, taking the balance from $11,920 to $46,920. At ~91% LVR, lenders apply strict **genuine savings** rules (typically 5% of purchase price held/accumulated over 3+ months).

- This $35,000 landed **~10 days before** the statement close and is **not seasoned** — most lenders will not count it as genuine savings.
- Without it, genuine savings are only ~$11,520, which is **below the 5% genuine savings threshold** (5% of $720k = $36,000).
- The **source is undocumented**. If it's a gift, you'll need a **statutory declaration / gift letter** confirming it's non-repayable; if it's from another of Sarah's accounts, you need statements showing it accumulated there.
- **Action:** Get written evidence of the source of the $35,000. This directly affects whether the loan is approvable at this LVR.

## 2. Important issues (resolve before or at lodgement)

### 2.1 Only one payslip; payslip is incomplete
- Most lenders require **two consecutive recent payslips**; only the latest (21 Jul – 3 Aug 2026) is on file.
- The **tax figure is missing** (shown as "?"), so the payslip is incomplete. Gross $2,884.62 − net $2,150.00 implies ~$734.62 tax, but the document itself doesn't show it.
- Consider adding an **employment confirmation letter** or the most recent PAYG income statement to corroborate income and permanency.
- **Action:** Obtain a second payslip and a complete/legible copy showing the tax line.

Income sanity-check: $2,884.62 gross/fortnight × 26 ≈ **$75,000 p.a. gross**. Salary credits of $2,150 in the bank statement align with the net payslip figure — that's consistent. (Note: super shown is $331.73 ≈ 11.5%; the Super Guarantee rate from 1 Jul 2025 is 12% (~$346) — minor, worth a glance but not a blocker.)

### 2.2 Living expenses likely understated
- Declared living expenses are **$1,650/month** for a single applicant in metro Sydney, which is low and will likely be **floored to the lender's HEM benchmark**.
- The savings statement shows **no rent, groceries, utilities or general spending** — only salary in and credit-card payments out. That suggests day-to-day spending runs through **another account** that hasn't been provided, or she lives rent-free (e.g. with family). Either way the true expense position isn't evidenced.
- **Action:** Confirm her actual living situation and expenses, and obtain the transaction account she uses for daily spending (lenders usually want 3 months of the primary spending account).

### 2.3 First Home Buyer benefits — check eligibility and assumptions
At a **$720,000** purchase in NSW:
- **First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme (transfer duty):** full exemption applies only up to $650k; **$650k–$800k is a partial concession**, so Sarah will pay **some** transfer duty — not zero. This must be built into the funds-to-complete figure (see 1.2).
- **First Home Owner Grant ($10k):** generally limited to **new homes/newly built**. 5/22 Hill Road appears to be an **established strata unit**, so the FHOG is **likely not available**.
- Her stated objective ("lowest ongoing repayment", move in before year-end) plus 91% LVR suggests she may be assuming full FHB concessions — verify so expectations and the cash position are correct.
- **Action:** Confirm FHB scheme eligibility and reflect the actual duty payable in the funds calculation.

### 2.4 Strata property costs not captured
The property is a **unit (5/22 Hill Road)**, so there will be **strata/body-corporate levies** plus council and water rates. These are ongoing commitments that feed serviceability and aren't recorded anywhere in the file.
- **Action:** Obtain strata levy amount (from the contract's strata disclosure / s184 certificate) and council/water rates, and include them in servicing.
