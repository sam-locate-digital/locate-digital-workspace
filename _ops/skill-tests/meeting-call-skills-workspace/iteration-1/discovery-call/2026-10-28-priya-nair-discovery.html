Discovery questions

  1. Can you walk me through how a loan file moves through the office, from first conversation to lodgement? Who touches it along the way?
    Probe: Of the 7, how many are brokers and how many are processors or admin?
    Listen for: the team split, where the processors sit in the file, and whether she knows the workflow in detail or has stepped back from it.
  2. Tell me about the last time you saw one of the processors using ChatGPT. What were they doing with it?
    Probe: Was that the free version on their own account, or something the firm set up?
    Listen for: payslips, bank statements, ID or client names being pasted in; personal accounts; whether there's any firm-provided tool (aggregator AI, Copilot).
  3. What made this feel like a problem now rather than six months ago?
    Probe: Has anything happened, like a question from your aggregator, an audit, or a near-miss on a file?
    Listen for: a trigger with a date attached. A trigger means urgency; "just a feeling" means a slower decision.
  4. If you had to guess, how much of a processor's week goes on checking documents and keying data?
    Probe: Is that where they say ChatGPT is helping?
    Listen for: her own number. That's the time saving the workshop is built around, and it's worth more coming from her than from me.
  5. Have you tried anything so far, like a rule, a staff email, a training session through your aggregator?
    Probe: How did the team take it?
    Listen for: a ban that's being ignored (common), aggregator material she found too generic, or nothing yet.
  6. You mentioned the Facebook ads aren't doing much. Who looks after them, and what do you spend in a typical month?
    Probe: Roughly how many enquiries a month come from them, and how many turn into an appointment?
    Listen for: monthly spend, whether anyone tracks enquiries to settlements, and whether this is a real priority or a passing comment. Don't diagnose on the call.
  7. Who holds the credit licence for the firm, and who'd need to be comfortable with a written AI use policy?
    Probe: Are you a credit rep under an aggregator? Would they want to see the policy?
    Listen for: whether Priya can sign off alone, and whether the licensee or aggregator needs to be involved.
  8. If this went ahead, when could you realistically get all 7 people in a room for half a day?
    Probe: Are there any months that are off limits, like end of financial year?
    Listen for: a specific window in the next 4–8 weeks (green) or "sometime next year" (amber).
