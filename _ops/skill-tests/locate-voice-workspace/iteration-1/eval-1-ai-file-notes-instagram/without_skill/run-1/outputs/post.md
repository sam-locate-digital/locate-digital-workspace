# Instagram post: AI-drafted file notes for mortgage brokers

## Caption

Most brokers I talk to don't hate writing file notes. They hate writing them at 9pm, three days after the meeting, trying to remember why the client picked the offset over the fixed split.

That's where AI has actually been useful.

Here's the workflow I've been setting up with brokers:

1. Record the client meeting (with consent) or dictate a quick voice memo straight after.
2. AI turns it into a structured draft: client objectives, needs and priorities, options considered, why the recommended product fits, and anything still outstanding.
3. You read it, fix what's wrong, add your judgement, and save it to the CRM.

Ten minutes of admin becomes two. And the notes are written while the conversation is still fresh, which is exactly what you want if a file ever gets reviewed.

A few things that don't change:

- You're still the one accountable under Best Interests Duty. The AI drafts, you decide and sign off.
- Client data needs to go into a tool that doesn't train on it and keeps it stored appropriately. Check this before you paste anything in.
- If the draft says something the client didn't say, delete it. Never let it fill gaps with guesses.

Done properly, it's not a shortcut. It's better documentation with less of your evening gone.
