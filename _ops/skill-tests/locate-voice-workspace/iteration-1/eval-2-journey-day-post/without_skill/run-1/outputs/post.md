# Instagram + Facebook post: tomorrow's day

## Caption

Tomorrow's a big one.

6am: gym. Non-negotiable. It's the only hour of the day nobody needs anything from me.

Morning: setting up Claude for an accounting firm's whole team. Accounts, permissions, and the boring-but-important bit, which is making sure client data stays where it should.

Arvo: their first training session. No buzzwords and no "AI will replace you" talk. We'll take the jobs they already do every week (drafting client emails, summarising long documents, getting a first pass on file notes) and show them how to get through them faster.

I've found the setup is the easy part. Whether a team actually uses it on a Tuesday afternoon comes down to that first session.

A year ago I wouldn't have guessed this is where Locate would be. Now it's some of my favourite work.

If your team has AI licences sitting there unused, or you haven't worked out where to start, send me a message.
