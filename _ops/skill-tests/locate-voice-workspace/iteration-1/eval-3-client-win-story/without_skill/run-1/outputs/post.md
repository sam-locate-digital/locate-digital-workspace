# Client win post: Brisbane financial planning firm

**Platform:** Instagram / Facebook (Locate Digital)

---

4 booked meetings a month. Now 11.

No new ad spend. No new website. We changed two things.

A financial planning firm in Brisbane came to us because their enquiries had gone quiet. The traffic was there and people were landing on the site. They just weren't getting through to a meeting.

Here's what we found.

**1. The enquiry form was a job interview.**
14 fields. Income, super balance, assets, goals, preferred adviser, how did you hear about us. Someone thinking "maybe I should talk to a planner" doesn't want to hand over their whole financial life to a website at 9pm.

We cut it to 4: name, email, phone, and one question, "What's on your mind?" Everything else can be asked in the first meeting, where it belongs.

**2. Follow-up was taking 2 to 3 days.**
By then most people had cooled off, forgotten they enquired, or booked with someone else.

We set up a same-day follow-up email that goes out within the hour. It's short, it's from a real adviser, it says what the first meeting looks like, and it has a booking link. If they don't book, a second email lands two days later.

**The result:** 4 booked meetings a month became 11. Same traffic. Same team. Nearly three times the conversations.

Most firms don't have a lead problem. They have a friction problem. The people are already showing up. You're just making it hard for them to say yes.

If your enquiries feel slow, look at your form and time how long your follow-up takes before you spend another dollar on ads.
