# Client access guide
**Locate Digital — for new clients**

---

This guide covers exactly how to give Locate Digital access to your accounts. Each section has step-by-step instructions written for someone who doesn't live in these platforms every day.

If you get stuck on any step, send a screenshot to sam@locatedigital.io and we'll sort it out.

The email to add for all platforms is: **sam@locatedigital.io**

## What we need access to

Depending on what we're working on together, we'll need some or all of the following:

- Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads)
- Google Ads — paid search campaigns
- Google Analytics — website traffic data
- Google Business Profile — your Google Maps listing
- Your CRM or booking system — appointment and patient data (if applicable)

You don't need to do all of these at once. We'll tell you which ones apply to your engagement.

## Meta

**What it is:** The central hub for your Facebook page, Instagram account, ad account, and pixel.

> **Note:** If your business doesn't have a Meta Business Manager account yet, go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) and create one before following the steps below. It takes about 5 minutes.

### Add us to your Business Manager

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com)
2. Click **Settings** (gear icon, left sidebar)
3. Under **Users**, click **People**
4. Click **Add people**
5. Enter **sam@locatedigital.io**
6. Set role to **Admin**
7. Click **Next**, then assign access to your **Ad Account**, **Facebook Page**, and **Instagram Account**
8. Click **Invite**

### Give us access to your Pixel (if one exists)

1. In Business Manager, go to **Settings → Data sources → Pixels**
2. Click on your pixel
3. Click **Add people**, enter **sam@locatedigital.io**, set to **Admin**, click **Save**

If you don't have a pixel yet, don't worry — we'll create one and install it.

## Google Ads

There are two ways to do this. Option A is preferred — it keeps your account under your ownership and lets us manage it from our end without needing your login.

### Option A — Link to our manager account (preferred)

1. Go to [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com)
2. Sign in to your Google Ads account
3. Click the **tools icon** (wrench) in the top-right corner
4. Under **Setup**, click **Access and security**
5. Click the **Managers** tab
6. Click **+** to send a link request — we'll send you our manager account ID separately and you enter it here
7. Click **Send request**

We'll accept from our end within 24 hours.

### Option B — Add as a user directly

1. Go to [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com) and sign in
2. Click the **tools icon** (wrench) → **Access and security**
3. Click the **Users** tab, then the **+** button
4. Enter **sam@locatedigital.io**
5. Set access level to **Admin**
6. Click **Send invitation**

## Google Analytics

**What it is:** Tracks who visits your website, where they come from, and what they do.

1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Sign in with the Google account that owns the property
3. Click the **gear icon** (Admin) in the bottom-left corner
4. Under the **Property** column, click **Property access management**
5. Click the blue **+** button in the top-right corner, then **Add users**
6. Enter **sam@locatedigital.io**
7. Under Role, select **Editor**
8. Click **Add**

---

## Google Business Profile

**What it is:** Your listing on Google Maps and Google Search — the panel that shows your address, hours, phone number, and reviews.

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in and select your business
3. Click **Menu** (three lines, top-left) → **Business Profile settings**
4. Click **Managers**
5. Click the **Add** button (person icon with a +)
6. Enter **sam@locatedigital.io**
7. Set role to **Manager**
8. Click **Invite**

## How long does this take?

Most people get through all platforms in 20–30 minutes. The steps look long written out but each one is just a few clicks.

The only thing that can slow it down is if someone else controls the account — an old agency, a web developer, or an IT contact. If that's the case, let us know and we can help you chase the right person.
