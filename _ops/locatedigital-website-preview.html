A bit of background

11 years in-house before going out on my own

I spent 11 years doing digital marketing inside businesses. Five years in FMCG. Competitive markets, tight budgets, always justifying spend. Then six years in financial services across investment, real estate, wealth management, and lending.

In those roles I ran campaigns myself, managed external agencies, and built strategy from scratch. I know how agencies work because I used to be the client managing them. I know the difference between an agency that's delivering and one that's just keeping the account warm.

Locate Digital came from a simple observation: small businesses need good digital marketing just as much as big ones. Most just can't access it at a price that makes sense, or find an agency that actually gets what running a service business involves.

Services

We focus on what actually generates leads for service businesses. And we're straight about what's right for your situation and what isn't.

Paid media

Dominate your market with paid ads

Google Ads and Meta Ads, built to convert. If you're spending on paid ads and not sure what you're getting back, something's off. Ads should have clear inputs and measurable outputs.

We build campaigns around your actual business goals. Cost per lead, not cost per click. We report on what matters, not what fills a slide deck.

  • Google Ads: Search, Display, Performance Max
  • Meta Ads: Facebook and Instagram
  • Campaign setup and ongoing optimisation
  • Conversion tracking setup
  • Monthly reporting in plain English

SEO

Search engine optimisation

Most service businesses depend on Google Search more than they realise. If your site doesn't show up when someone searches for what you do in your area, you're giving those leads to whoever does rank.

SEO takes longer than paid ads, but it compounds. We handle the technical work, local signals, and content. The things that actually shift rankings for service businesses.

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes
  • Google Business Profile optimisation
  • Local SEO: suburb and city targeting
  • On-page content optimisation
  • Monthly performance reporting

Social media management

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

Inconsistent posting does more damage than no posting. It signals to potential clients that the business isn't active, isn't paying attention, or isn't worth following.

We handle the whole thing. Strategy, content creation, scheduling, community management, and paid social where it makes sense. You approve the content calendar and we handle the rest.

  • Platform strategy
  • Content creation: captions, graphics, short-form video
  • Scheduling and posting
  • Comments and DM management
  • Meta Ads integration where relevant
  • Monthly content calendar for approval

Email marketing

Stay front of mind. Automatically.

Email is still one of the highest-return channels for service businesses. Most leads don't convert first time. Email keeps you in front of them until they're ready. And for existing clients, it's the best way to drive repeat bookings and referrals.

We set up the automations that run without you, and manage campaigns when you need them.

  • Email platform setup or audit
  • Automated sequence copywriting and setup
  • Campaign emails: newsletters, promotions, follow-ups
  • List segmentation
  • Reporting on opens, clicks, and conversions

Digital strategy

Success online requires a clear strategy

If you're running a few different marketing activities and can't tell what's working, the problem is usually the absence of a plan that ties it together. You end up spending across channels without a clear picture of which ones are pulling their weight.

A strategy engagement starts with an honest audit of where you are. Then we build a plan: which channels to focus on, what the budget should look like, and what to expect at 90 days and 12 months.

  • Audit of existing marketing activity
  • Competitor and market review
  • Channel prioritisation and budget recommendation
  • 90-day action plan
  • Ongoing advisory check-ins
