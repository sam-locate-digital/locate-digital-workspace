### Sam's story

**H2:** A bit of background

I spent 11 years doing digital marketing in-house before starting Locate Digital. The first five were in FMCG — consumer goods, competitive markets, tight budgets. The next six were in financial services, covering investment, real estate, wealth management, and lending.

In those roles I ran campaigns myself, built marketing strategies from scratch, and managed external agencies when we needed specialist support. I know how agencies work from the inside. I know the shortcuts they take and the metrics that actually matter to a business owner versus the ones that look good in a slide deck.

Starting Locate Digital wasn't a big career pivot. It was a logical next step. Small and medium businesses need proper digital marketing — the kind that's clear, honest, and tied to real outcomes. Most of them don't have an in-house team to run it, and not all agencies are set up to serve businesses at that scale properly.

That's the gap we fill.

---

### What we believe

**H2:** How we think about marketing

Good marketing isn't complicated. It's being in the right place, in front of the right people, with a message that makes them want to take action. Everything else is execution.

We don't believe in locking clients into long contracts they can't get out of. We don't believe in reporting that buries performance in impressive-looking numbers. And we don't believe in pitching services you don't need.

What we do believe in: knowing your numbers, understanding your customers, and building marketing that compounds over time.

---

### Who we work with

**H2:** Who we work with

We work with service businesses — the kind where the owner is usually close to the work and doesn't have time to become a marketing expert on top of everything else.

Healthcare clinics, professional services firms, financial advisers, trades, fitness studios. If you run a service business and you need more of the right clients finding you, we can help.

We tend to be a good fit for businesses that have some marketing activity already but aren't sure it's working as hard as it should — and want someone to look at it clearly and tell them the truth.

### Service 1 — Paid media (Google Ads + Meta Ads)

**H2:** Paid media

If you're spending on Google or Meta and not sure what you're getting for it, something's off. Paid ads should have clear inputs and measurable outputs.

We build and manage campaigns that are set up to convert — the right audiences, the right messaging, the right bidding strategy. We track cost per lead, not just clicks. And we report on what's actually happening, not what looks impressive.

**What's included:**
- Google Ads (Search, Display, Performance Max)
- Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)
- Campaign setup and ongoing optimisation
- Conversion tracking setup
- Monthly reporting in plain language

---

### Service 2 — SEO

**H2:** Search engine optimisation

Most service businesses rely on Google Search more than they realise. If your website doesn't come up when someone searches for what you do in your area, you're handing leads to whoever does.

SEO is a longer game than paid ads, but it builds something that lasts. We handle the technical foundation, local search optimisation, and content strategy — the three things that move the needle for service businesses.

**What's included:**
- Technical SEO audit and fixes
- Google Business Profile optimisation
- Local SEO (suburb and city targeting)
- On-page content optimisation
- Monthly performance reporting

---

### Service 3 — Social media management

**H2:** Social media management

Most service businesses know they should be more consistent on social. The problem is it takes time you don't have, and inconsistent posting does more damage than no posting at all.

We handle it end to end. Strategy, content creation, scheduling, and paid social if needed. You stay in the loop on what's going out, but it doesn't sit on your to-do list.

**What's included:**
- Platform strategy (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- Content creation — captions, graphics, short-form video
- Scheduling and posting
- Community management (comments and DMs)
- Meta Ads integration where relevant
- Monthly content calendar for your review

---

### Service 4 — Email marketing

**H2:** Email marketing

Email is still one of the highest-return channels for service businesses — especially for staying in front of existing clients and warming up leads who aren't ready to book yet.

We set up the automations that run without you touching them (welcome sequences, re-engagement, follow-ups) and manage ongoing campaigns when you need them.

**What's included:**
- Email platform setup or audit
- Automated sequence design and copywriting
- Campaign emails (newsletters, promotions, announcements)
- List management and segmentation
- Performance reporting (opens, clicks, conversions)

---

### Service 5 — Digital strategy

**H2:** Digital strategy

If you're spending on marketing across a few channels but can't tell what's working, the problem is usually the absence of a plan that ties it all together.

A strategy engagement starts with an honest look at where you are — what's running, what the data says, where the gaps are. From there we build a clear plan: which channels to prioritise, what the budget should look like, and what success looks like at 90 days and 12 months.

**What's included:**
- Audit of existing marketing activity
- Competitor and market review
- Channel prioritisation and budget recommendation
- 90-day action plan
- Ongoing advisory (monthly or quarterly check-ins)
