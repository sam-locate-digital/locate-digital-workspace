# Your new website: what we need from you
**Locate Digital — for new website clients**

---

This guide covers everything you need to do while we build your website, and after it goes live. It's written for someone who'd rather be on the tools than in a browser. Most of it takes a few minutes.

If you get stuck on any step, send a screenshot to sam@locatedigital.io and we'll sort it out.

**One rule throughout: never send us a password.** Everything below uses invites, or a quick screen-share where you log in and we talk you through it.

## Before kickoff: what to gather

Send these to sam@locatedigital.io, or share a Google Drive or Dropbox folder. Don't worry if a few are missing: bring what you have to the interview.

### Your business details
- [ ] Your business name as you want it shown, and your full trading name
- [ ] Your ABN
- [ ] Your licence numbers: plumbing licence, electrical contractor licence (REC), or both
- [ ] Your public liability insurance cover (for example "$20 million")
- [ ] Your business phone number and the email address you want bookings sent to
- [ ] Your street address, or tell us if you'd rather show only your suburb
- [ ] Your hours, and whether you take emergency call-outs (and when)
- [ ] The year you started

### Your services and suburbs
- [ ] Every service you do. Then mark the three or four that make you the most money. These get their own pages.
- [ ] The suburbs you cover, in order of how much work comes from each. The top 16 get their own pages.
- [ ] Anything you don't do, so we never advertise it

### Your team
- [ ] Each person's name and role, as you'd like them shown

### Your logo
- [ ] The best file you have. Ask whoever designed it for a file ending in **.svg** or **.png** with a clear background. A photo of your van or a screenshot is a last resort, but we can work with it.

### Your photos (the most important part)
Real photos of you and your work beat stock photos every time. Send 15–30 if you can. Phone photos are fine.

- **Do:** take them in landscape (phone sideways), in daylight, with the area tidy
- **Do:** get people in them: you or the team at work, talking to a customer, beside the van
- **Shots we need most:**
  - [ ] You or the team on a job inside a home (this is your main photo)
  - [ ] The team beside the van or ute
  - [ ] One or two of each main service: a hot water unit, a switchboard, a drain, a finished bathroom
  - [ ] A clean, finished job
- **Don't:** send photos of customers' faces or homes without their OK, or photos you got from another business or the internet

### Your reviews
- [ ] Nothing to send. We'll pick 6–9 of your real Google reviews and send you the list to approve before they go on the site.

## The kickoff interview (45 minutes)

We'll ask about:
- how the business started, and what you do differently from other tradies
- the jobs you want more of, and the ones you'd rather not do
- where your best customers are
- whether you work for landlords and property managers
- how fast you can usually get back to a booking
- your current website, if you have one, and where your domain and email are set up

There are no wrong answers. The way you'd explain it to a customer is exactly what we want to hear, because it becomes the words on your site.

## Giving us access

### Your domain (your web address)
Your domain is your web address, like `smithplumbing.com.au`. To launch, we change two settings on it so it points at your new website. Your email isn't touched.

**First, find where it's registered.** Search your email for a renewal receipt with your domain name in it. Common Australian companies are VentraIP, Crazy Domains, GoDaddy, Netregistry and IONOS. If a past web designer set it up, ask them. If you can't find it, tell us the domain and we'll look it up.

**Then pick one:**
- **Option A (easiest):** book a 15-minute screen-share with us. You log in, we tell you exactly what to click.
- **Option B:** we email you the two settings (called "DNS records") and you add them yourself. We'll send step-by-step instructions for your company.
- **Option C:** some companies let you add another person to your account. If yours does, add sam@locatedigital.io.

**If you don't have a domain yet:** we'll help you register one in **your business name and ABN**, paid by you, so it always belongs to you. It costs around $20–40 a year.

### Google Business Profile, Google Analytics and Google Ads
Follow our [client access guide](client-access-guide.md). It has step-by-step instructions for each one:
- **Google Business Profile:** needed for every website, so we can link it to your new site
- **Google Analytics:** if you don't have one, we'll create it in your name and invite you as the owner
- **Google Ads:** only if you've chosen the Website and Google plan. You add your own card for ad spend inside Google Ads: we never handle it

### The booking email
Tell us which inbox bookings should go to. Use one you check during the day on your phone.

## Reviewing your site

1. We'll send you a **preview link**. It looks exactly like the finished site, just on a temporary address.
2. Look at it **on your phone first**, the way most customers will.
3. Send us **one list of changes** per round, in a single email. Screenshots with a note are perfect ("change this to…").
4. You get **two rounds of changes**. We'll make the first list, send the link back, then make the second.
5. When you're happy, reply "approved for launch".

Changes to words, photos and details are all part of your two rounds. A new page or a big new section is quoted separately, and we'll always tell you before doing it.

## Launch day

- We connect your domain and the new site goes live, usually within an hour.
- Your email keeps working as normal. We only change the settings for the website, and we'll send you a test email to check.
- **Please don't change any settings on your domain yourself** around launch.
- **Don't cancel your old website hosting yet.** In some setups it also runs your email. We'll tell you when it's safe, about two weeks after launch.

## After launch

### Bookings
- Each booking arrives in your inbox with the job, the suburb, the customer's mobile and any notes.
- **Reply quickly.** Your site tells customers you'll text them a time within [your response time], so text them back within that time. Fast replies win the job.
- The first booking email may land in spam. If it does, mark it "not spam" so the rest arrive in your inbox.
- Keep notifications on for that inbox on your phone.

### Getting more reviews
Ask happy customers for a Google review. We'll send you your direct review link to text them after a job. Each good review helps you show up higher on Google, and we add the best ones to your site.

### Changes to your site
Email sam@locatedigital.io with what you'd like changed. Your care plan covers small edits each month: new reviews, hours, a special offer, swapping a photo. Bigger changes, like a new page, we'll quote first.

### Your monthly email
Each month we'll send a short update: how many calls and bookings came from the site, and any changes we made.
