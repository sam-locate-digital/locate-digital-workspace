# Social post — client feed content

Write Instagram and Facebook content for Locate Digital clients. Organic, patient/client-acquisition focused, local service business tone.

Covers:
- Instagram feed posts (static images, carousels)
- Facebook feed posts

**Not this skill:** Sam's own brand Reels → use `reels-script`. Instagram Reels scripts for clients → ask Sam if needed.

## Client context (read first)

Read the client's `CLAUDE.md` from `clients/<name>/CLAUDE.md`. Pull:
- Business name, industry, locations
- Target audience
- Services to promote
- Voice/tone notes
- Any content restrictions (medical, legal — apply disclaimer rules below)

If no client is specified, ask which client this is for.

## Inputs (ask only if missing)

- **Platform** — Instagram, Facebook, or both?
- **Post type** — educational, seasonal, social proof, service spotlight, or promotional?
- **Topic** — what's the subject? (e.g. "winter desk pain", "EOFY for accountants", "running injuries")
- **Number of posts** — one-off or a batch?

If only "write a social post for [client]" → propose 3 angles, let Sam pick.

## Post types

**Educational** — practical knowledge, builds trust, generates saves. Best performing for service businesses.
Format: Problem → Insight → Practical tip. Must be genuinely useful, not generic.

**Seasonal/timely** — ties to a real moment: EOFY, AFL season, Melbourne Marathon, school holidays, winter stiffness.

**Social proof** — patient/client outcomes (anonymised), Google review quote, milestone number. Specific beats vague.

**Service spotlight** — one service explained plainly. Who it's for, what a session involves, what to expect. Demystify over sell.

**Promotional** — new patient offer, referral incentive. Keep tone informational. Use sparingly.

## Instagram feed post

```
CAPTION:
[Hook — 1–2 lines. Direct callout or surprising insight. No "hey guys" or "exciting news".]

[Body — 2–4 short paragraphs. Max 4 lines per paragraph. Real value, not filler.]

[CTA — 1 line. Specific: "Book via the link in bio" / "Call us on [number]" / "DM us the word [X]"]

HASHTAGS (8–12):
[2–3 local: #melbournecbd #essendon #melbournehealth]
[2–3 industry: #osteopathy #backpain #runninginjury]
[2–3 niche: #deskpain #corporatehealth #melbournemarathon]
[1–2 broad: #smallbusinessmelbourne #melbournelife]

VISUAL DIRECTION:
[Specific — subject, mood, composition. Not "professional clinic photo".]
```

## Facebook post

```
POST:
[Hook — 1–2 lines]

[Body — 2–4 short paragraphs. Slightly more explanatory than Instagram. Facebook readers tolerate more text.]

[CTA — include phone number or booking link in the post body, not just a comment.]

HASHTAGS (3–5 only — Facebook hashtags have low impact, don't over-stack):
[local + 1–2 topical]

VISUAL DIRECTION:
[Same as Instagram]
```

## AU English and local signals

- Australian spelling throughout
- Name Melbourne suburbs and landmarks where relevant
- Reference AU-specific context: EOFY, AFL, AHPRA (health), Xero (accounting), Cliniko (health), Medicare
- Recommended posting times for AU audiences: 7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–9pm AEDT

## What to avoid

- "Follow us for more tips" (kills organic reach)
- Excessive hashtag stacking (30-tag dumps hurt Instagram reach)
- Generic advice that could apply to any business anywhere
- Emoji overuse — one or two max, only if they add meaning
- US references, non-AU context
- "Exciting news!" openers

## Medical and professional services

Health clients (osteos, physios, dentists): no outcome guarantees. Use "may help", "commonly used to treat", "many patients find". AHPRA compliance applies.

Legal/accounting clients: no specific tax or legal advice in captions. General service descriptions only. Direct to consult.

## Humanizer pass (always last)

Run humanizer on the caption before delivering. Focus on:
- Removing AI vocabulary and filler
- Making it sound like the business actually wrote it
- Varying sentence length
- Sentence case for any pull-out lines or sub-headers

Deliver humanized post as final output.
