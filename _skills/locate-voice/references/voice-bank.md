# Voice bank: what Locate does, in its own words

Source: locatedigital.io (the October 2026 site rebuild, `_brand/site/` in the Locate workspace). If the site changes, re-read it before trusting this file.

## Positioning

- Marketing and practical AI for Australian financial service providers.
- "One industry, understood in depth." Locate only works with firms that hold a licence, handle client money or give advice.
- Why finance: Sam spent most of their career in financial services and saw good firms poorly served by agencies that treated them like retailers. Campaigns written like retail ads, content that didn't sound like a trusted firm, reporting that counted clicks while principals wanted to know how many clients signed, and nobody helping them use AI safely.
- The shared challenges of these firms: long decision cycles, competitive markets, clients who need to trust you before they make contact, teams stretched thin by admin.
- How their clients decide: research for weeks or months, compare options, usually act after a change in circumstances. The firm that is visible, credible and quick to respond at that moment usually gets the conversation.
- Core promise: growth that does not depend on referrals alone. A second source of qualified enquiries the firm controls, and repeat admin taken off the team.

## Services

**Marketing** (each run by a senior specialist, inside accounts the firm owns, reported monthly in plain English)
- Paid ads: Google, Meta and LinkedIn planned as one plan. Search captures people looking for what you offer, social builds familiarity, LinkedIn reaches decision-makers. Measured on enquiries.
- Conversion rate optimisation: find where people drop off between landing on the site and getting in touch, then fix it. Clearer pages, simpler forms, faster load times. Disclaimers and calculators checked for accuracy and required disclosures.
- Email: nurture sequences, newsletters, review reminders. Keeps the firm in the conversation between first contact and signed engagement, and brings existing clients back. Spam Act compliant.

**AI** (no technical background needed)
- AI integrations and training: Claude set up inside the email, documents and tools the firm already uses; hands-on training by role, on the team's own work; a prompt and skills library; ongoing coaching. Built on Claude because it is careful with detail, strong at writing and reasoning, and easy for anyone to use.
- AI automation: workflows that draft file notes, summarise enquiries, chase documents and prepare follow-ups. A person stays in charge of anything that reaches a client.
- AI Readiness Assessment: 10 questions, about 5 minutes, score and recommendations on screen. Nothing is sent until the firm chooses to book. Good soft CTA.

## Operating principles (usable as post material)

- Finance specialists: we already understand your clients and how they decide.
- Marketing and AI under one roof: campaigns and automation planned together, so new enquiries are followed up quickly and nothing falls through the gaps.
- Clear plans, no guesswork: every engagement starts with a written 90-day plan.
- You own your accounts and data: ad accounts, analytics, automations and AI workspaces sit in the firm's name. If we part ways, everything stays with you.
- Measured on results: enquiries, meetings and clients won, in plain English, every month.
- A named strategist you can call, not a ticket queue.
- AI prepares the work. Your people make the decisions that matter.
- Start with one workflow that saves real time each week, prove it with the team, then extend.

## The Locate method

Four stages, same order for every firm, each ending with something the firm can see and approve before moving on.
