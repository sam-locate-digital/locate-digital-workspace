# Fonts

## Avoid

| Font | Why |
|---|---|
| Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Manrope, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Figtree | Defaults in AI site builders and templates; instantly read as generated |
| Fraunces, Instrument Serif | The "trendy serif" picks of AI-built and template sites |
| Styrene, Tiempos | Anthropic / Claude brand fonts |
| Söhne | OpenAI brand font |

IBM Plex Sans is common on tech and AI sites too. It isn't banned, but don't pick it as a default.

## Less common pairings that suit a professional-services brand

All are free on Google Fonts, so they load the same way as any other web font.

1. **Newsreader + Public Sans.** An editorial serif for headings with a plain, neutral sans for body. Reads as an established firm rather than a startup; the strongest break from the generated look. Locate Digital's own site uses this.
   - Newsreader: headings 600, labels 400; tracking about −0.015em on headings.
   - Public Sans: body, buttons, navigation, forms (400–700).
2. **Host Grotesk + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.** A modern all-sans option in fonts that are rarely seen. Atkinson was designed for readability, which gives an accessibility story.
3. **Mona Sans (wide) + Mona Sans.** One family used two ways: a wider cut for headings, normal width for body.
4. **Libre Franklin + Literata.** A classic newspaper sans for headings with a serif body. The most conservative and "financial" option.

For small "system" labels, if a monospace is wanted: Fragment Mono or Martian Mono. Avoid DM Mono, Geist Mono, Space Mono and JetBrains Mono, which are common on generated and developer sites.

## Choosing

- Show the shortlist as specimens with the client's real headline and body text in their brand colour, plus the current font for comparison, before building.
- If a serif goes on headings, keep buttons, navigation and forms in the sans. Serif buttons look wrong.
- Serif headings need lighter weight (about 600) and looser tracking than a grotesk at 800. Copying grotesk heading settings onto a serif cramps it.
- When swapping fonts on an existing site, check everything that inherits the heading font, such as big stat numbers and display paragraphs, not just h1 and h2.
