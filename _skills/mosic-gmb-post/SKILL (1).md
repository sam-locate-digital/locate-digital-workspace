# MOSIC — Google My Business Post Generator

Generate two informational Google My Business posts for MOSIC per fortnight. Each post must be under 1,500 characters total. SEO-optimised for local Melbourne search. Warm, professional tone aimed at male professionals aged 25–55.

## Business context

**Clinic:** Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre (MOSIC)
**Locations:** Level 9, 140 Bourke St Melbourne CBD | 320–324 Keilor Rd Essendon North
**Phone:** 03 9663 6202
**Online booking:** Available at melbourneosteopathycentre.com.au
**Telehealth:** Also available
**Services to prioritise:** Osteopathy, running injuries, work injuries, corporate health, general health

## Target audience

Male professionals aged 25–55 working in or near Melbourne CBD or Essendon. They sit at desks for long hours, commute, and play sport on weekends. They're searching when something already hurts — back pain, neck pain, a niggling sports injury they've put off too long. They search phrases like "osteopath Melbourne CBD", "back pain Bourke Street", "neck pain treatment Melbourne", "sports injury clinic Melbourne".

## SEO keywords — use naturally, not forced

Primary: osteopath Melbourne, Melbourne CBD, Bourke Street
Secondary: back pain, neck pain, sports injury, work injury, osteopathy treatment
Local signals: Bourke Street, Melbourne CBD, Essendon, CBD professionals, city workers

## Post structure

Every post follows this four-part flow:

1. **Hook** (1–2 sentences) — open with the problem or a surprising insight. Reference Melbourne context where it fits naturally (season, Melbourne events, CBD lifestyle).
2. **Body** (3–5 sentences) — explain why it matters and what osteopathy does about it. Work in 1–2 primary keywords. Be informative, not salesy.
3. **Local anchor** (1 sentence) — ground it in place. E.g. "Our Bourke Street and Essendon clinics regularly treat CBD professionals with exactly this pattern."
4. **CTA** (1 sentence) — always include both channels: "Book online at melbourneosteopathycentre.com.au or call 03 9663 6202."

## Hard rules

- **Under 1,500 characters total including spaces** — count at the end and trim body if needed
- Purely informational — no promotional language, offers, discounts, or events
- No bullet points or headers — GMB renders plain text only, so write in flowing prose
- Australian English throughout (practise not practice, programme not program)
- Never mention competitors
- Clinic voice, not patient voice

## Two posts per fortnight

Each fortnightly content run generates two GMB posts — one for each week. Both share the same broad topic as the blog post, but take slightly different angles:
- Post 1: focus on the problem / what's causing it
- Post 2: focus on the solution / what MOSIC does about it

Both must be under 1,500 characters. Both go through the humanizer pass before saving.

## Output format

For each post deliver three things:

1. **GMB post** — plain text, no markdown, no line breaks between structural parts (just natural paragraph flow)
2. **Character count** — exact count including spaces
3. **Suggested photo caption** — one specific sentence describing an ideal accompanying image (subject, mood, setting — enough detail that someone can find or shoot it)

## Humanizer pass (always run last)

After generating both GMB posts, run the humanizer skill on each post text. Follow the humanizer process: draft rewrite, AI-tell audit, final version. Recount characters after humanizing and confirm still under 1,500. Save only the final humanized versions. Do not skip this step.
