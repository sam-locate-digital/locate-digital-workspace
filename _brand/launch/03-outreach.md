# Outreach — Locate Digital

The whole early game is **volume + personalisation + a free audit hook.** Your goal: 10 personalised approaches a day until you have 3 clients.

## The offer (your hook)

A free, no-strings **"3-minute ad teardown"** — a short Loom video where you screen-record their current ads/website/Google listing and point out 2–3 things costing them new patients. You do the work first, then send it. This beats any written pitch because it proves competence instead of claiming it.

## Channels, in priority order

1. **Warm intros** — anyone you already know in or near allied health. Highest conversion.
2. **DM / email + free teardown** — cold but personalised.
3. **Follow-up sequence** — most replies come on follow-up 2 and 3.

## Step 1 — Build the list

100 Melbourne physio/osteo clinics. Source from Google Maps ("physio Melbourne", "osteopath [suburb]"), Instagram, and the AHPRA/association directories. Capture: clinic name, owner name, email, IG handle, whether they're running ads (check Meta Ad Library), website.

## Step 2 — Warm message (for people you know)

> Hey [name], quick one — I've just started Locate Digital, helping Melbourne physio and osteo clinics get more new patients through Google and Meta ads. Not pitching you, but do you know any clinic owners who'd want a free 3-min review of their ads? Happy to just hand it over.

## Step 3 — Cold first touch (after you've recorded the teardown)

**Email subject:** Found 3 things costing [Clinic] new patients

> Hi [name],
>
> I had a look at [Clinic]'s ads and Google listing this morning and recorded a quick 3-minute video — spotted a few things that are likely costing you new patient bookings (one's an easy fix).
>
> No pitch, no catch. Here's the video: [Loom link]
>
> I run Locate Digital — we help Melbourne physio and osteo clinics book more new patients through Google and Meta. If any of it's useful, happy to chat. If not, keep the video.
>
> Cheers,
> Sam
> Locate Digital

**Instagram DM version (shorter):**

> Hi [name] — I help Melbourne physio/osteo clinics get more new patients through ads. Recorded a free 3-min teardown of [Clinic]'s current ads, spotted a couple of quick wins. Want me to send it over? No pitch.

## Step 4 — Follow-ups (this is where the replies are)

**Follow-up 1 (day 3):**
> Hi [name], did the teardown video come through ok? Happy to walk you through the fixes on a quick call if useful.

**Follow-up 2 (day 7):**
> Hey [name] — last one from me. The biggest thing I saw was [specific issue, e.g. "you're paying for clicks from people outside your catchment"]. Even if we never work together, worth fixing. Want me to send the how-to?

**Follow-up 3 (day 14):**
> Hi [name], I'll leave it here. If new patient numbers ever become a focus, I'm one message away. All the best with the clinic.

## Step 5 — On the call

1. Ask about their goals and current new-patient numbers before you pitch anything.
2. Walk through what you found in the teardown.
3. Recommend Growth tier. Frame the price against the value of one new patient's lifetime value (a physio patient is worth $400–1,500+ over their treatment).
4. Close on the $750 audit or a 3-month Growth retainer + $1,500 setup.

## Daily rhythm (until 3 clients)

- 10 new prospects added to the list
- 3–5 teardown videos recorded + sent
- All due follow-ups sent
- Track everything in a simple sheet: name, stage, last contact, next action

## First-client deal

Offer your first 1–2 clients a discounted rate **in exchange for a case study + testimonial.** Don't work free — trade price for proof. Client 3 onward pays full rate.
