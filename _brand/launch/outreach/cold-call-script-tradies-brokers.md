# Cold call script: tradies and mortgage brokers

For the 87 businesses in `2026-11-05-melbourne-weak-marketing-prospects.csv`. Read it off the screen. Bold lines are what you say. Everything else is for you.

---

## Read this once before you dial

**You are not selling a website or ads on this call.** You're selling one thing: a free 15-minute look at what they're losing and how to fix it. The meeting is the product. If you try to close the retainer on a cold call, you lose the call.

**The only goal of the call:** book the 15 minutes, or get permission to send the free thing. Nothing else counts.

**Why this works.** People don't buy marketing. They buy a bigger, surer, faster result with less effort and less risk. Every line below is built to do one of those:
- **Bigger result:** more jobs or more loans, in their words, in dollars.
- **More likely:** proof that's about *them* (their site, their gap), not about you.
- **Faster:** "live in two to three weeks", "first leads this month".
- **Less effort:** "I do all of it, you approve it in 10 minutes."
- **Less risk:** the first step is free, and they can say no after seeing it.

**The rules**
1. **Be specific in the first 20 seconds.** A specific problem *on their site* beats any pitch. The CSV gives you one for every business: the `Website status`, `Website issues` and `Marketing gaps` columns.
2. **Ask, don't tell.** Whoever asks the questions controls the call. Talk 30%, listen 70%.
3. **Get small yeses.** "Is now okay for 30 seconds?" "Fair to say most of your work comes from referrals?" Each yes makes the next one easier.
4. **Name the pain in dollars.** Ask what a job or a loan is worth to them, then do the maths out loud.
5. **Never argue with an objection.** Agree, reframe, ask a question.
6. **Sound like a tradie talking to a tradie.** Slow down, smile, keep it short. No "solutions", no "leverage".
7. **Volume wins.** Expect 1 in 10 to book from a good list. 50 dials a session, then review.

**Before each call (30 seconds)**
- Open their row in the CSV and their website.
- Pick the **one hook** from the table in section 2. Just one.
- If the site is BROKEN, screenshot it on your phone so you can text it straight after.
- Have demo.locatedigital.io/tradie or /mortgage-broker open.

## 1. Gatekeeper (office manager, receptionist, the owner's partner)

**"Hi, it's Sam from Locate Digital. Is [owner name / the owner] around?"**

If they ask what it's about:
**"I was looking at [Business]'s website this morning and found something that's probably costing you calls. It's a quick one. Who looks after the website and marketing there?"**

If they're not in:
**"No worries. When's the best time to catch them? And is it their mobile or this number I should try?"**
Get a name and a time. Write both in the CSV. Call back exactly then.

If they say "send an email":
**"Happy to. Who should it go to, and what's their direct email?"** Then send the 3-line email in section 7 within the hour.

## 2. The hooks (pick one from the CSV row)

## 3. The opener (both trades)

**"Hi [name], it's Sam from Locate Digital. I'll be quick, I know you're probably [on a job / between clients]. Have I caught you at an okay time for 30 seconds?"**

*(They'll usually say "what's it about?" That's a yes. Keep going.)*

**"I help [tradies / mortgage brokers] around Melbourne get more [jobs / clients] from Google. I was looking at [Business] this morning and noticed [THE HOOK]. Did you know about that?"**

Then **stop talking.** Let them answer. The silence does the work.

- **"Yeah, I know, I've been meaning to fix it."** → go to section 4.
- **"No, really?"** → **"Yeah, I can send you a screenshot after this. Can I ask you a couple of quick questions to see if what I do is even relevant to you?"** → section 4.
- **"Not interested."** → section 6.

---

## 4. Discovery questions (pick 3 or 4, ask them naturally)

These find out whether they're worth a meeting and build the pain. Write the answers down; you'll use their words later.

### Tradies
1. **"How many of you are on the tools at the moment?"** *(3 or more, or vans on the road, is a green light.)*
2. **"Where does most of your work come from right now? Referrals, builders, Google?"**
3. **"Are you as busy as you want to be, or are there quiet weeks?"**
4. **"What's your best kind of job, the one you'd want ten more of?"** *(Hot water, switchboards, reroofs, bathroom jobs. Let them get excited.)*
5. **"What's one of those jobs worth to you, roughly?"**
6. **"Has anyone run Google ads for you before? How'd that go?"**

### Mortgage brokers
1. **"How many brokers are in the team now?"**
2. **"Where are most of your new clients coming from? Referral partners, past clients, online?"**
3. **"If referrals went quiet for a month, what happens to your pipeline?"**
4. **"What's your ideal client, first home buyers, investors, refinancers, self-employed?"**
5. **"What does an average settled loan pay you in upfront and trail, roughly?"**
6. **"Have you tried ads before? What happened?"** *(Common answer: "got junk leads". Good. Note it.)*

### Do the maths out loud (this is the turn)

**"So if a [job / settled loan] is worth about [their number], and Google sent you even [3 / 2] more a month, that's [number × 3 or 2] a month, or about [× 12] a year you're not getting right now. Does that sound about right?"**

Get the yes. Now it's not a marketing chat. It's a money conversation.

---

## 5. The offer and the close (ask for the meeting, not the money)

**"Here's what I'd suggest. Let me do two things for you, free:**
**One, I'll put together a mockup of what your new homepage could look like, using your own business, so you can actually see it.**
**Two, I'll show you exactly what people in [suburb] are searching for, and who's getting those calls instead of you.**
**It takes me a couple of days. Then we jump on 15 minutes and I walk you through it. If you don't like it, no worries, you've got a free plan. Fair?"**

Then give two times. Never ask "when suits you?"
**"Are you better Tuesday morning or Thursday arvo?"**

Lock it in:
**"Perfect. I'll send you a calendar invite. What's the best email? And is this the best mobile for a text?"**

Confirm in the CSV: `Outcome = Booked`, with the date and time in `Follow-up`.

**For a BROKEN site**, make the free thing even smaller and faster:
**"Want me to text you the screenshot now so you can see what customers see? Then if it's useful, we'll grab 15 minutes and I'll show you how I'd fix it."**

### If they ask "how much?" on the call
Don't dodge it, but don't sell the price either. Give a range, then go back to the meeting.

**Tradies:** **"Websites start from about four and a half grand, and most tradies do the website plus Google package. But I genuinely don't know what you need until I've looked properly. That's what the 15 minutes is for. If it's not worth it, I'll tell you."**

**Brokers:** **"Depends on whether you need the site, the ads or both. Most of the work I do is a monthly retainer. I'd rather show you the plan first so you can see whether it's worth it, then we talk numbers."**

## 6. Objections (agree, reframe, ask)

**"Not interested."**
**"Totally fair, you didn't ask for my call. Can I ask, is it that you've got enough work, or that you've been burned by marketing people before?"**
*(Either answer gives you a door. If they've got enough work:)* **"Love that. When you're ready to pick and choose the better jobs, keep my number."**

**"I get all my work from word of mouth."**
**"That's the best kind of work, and it means you're good at what you do. The thing is, when someone gets referred to you, the first thing they do is Google you. Right now they find [THE HOOK]. I'm not trying to replace referrals. I'm making sure you don't lose the ones you've already earned."**

**"We're too busy."**
**"Good problem to have. Most busy [tradies / brokers] I talk to want better jobs, not more. Higher-value work, closer to home, fewer tyre kickers. Would that be useful?"**

**"Send me some info."**
**"Happy to. So I send you the right thing, what would you want it to show you?"** *(Then:)* **"Easiest is I send the free homepage mockup. Then if you like it, 15 minutes to walk through it. Fair?"**

**"I've got someone who does my website / ads."**
**"Good, that's the right move. Are you happy with how many calls it's bringing in? … I'm happy to give you a free second opinion. If they're doing a great job, I'll tell you, and you'll know you're in good hands."**

**"Tried Google ads before, waste of money."**
**"I hear that a lot, and most of the time it's the same three things: going after the wrong searches, sending people to a homepage that doesn't convert, and no tracking, so you can't tell what worked. I'd be keen to look at what went wrong. Even if you never work with me, you'll know why."**

**"How do I know you're any good?"**
**"You shouldn't take my word for it. That's why the first step is free. You see the plan and the mockup before you spend a cent, and if it's not obviously better than what you've got, don't go ahead."**

**"It's too expensive."** *(If price came up.)*
**"Compared to what? If it brings in [their number] more a month, what's that worth over a year? … I only want to work with you if the maths makes sense for you. Let's look at the plan and decide from there."**

**"Call me back later."**
**"Sure. Is that because now's a bad time, or because you're not sure it's relevant?"** *(If it's timing:)* **"What day and time works? I'll put it in my calendar."**

## 7. Voicemail and follow-up

**Voicemail (under 20 seconds, one go only):**
**"Hi [name], it's Sam from Locate Digital. I was looking at [Business]'s website and found [THE HOOK, short version]. I'll send you a text with a screenshot. My number's [your mobile]. Cheers."**

Then text straight away:
> Hi [name], Sam from Locate Digital. Just left you a voicemail. Here's what your website's showing right now [screenshot]. Happy to show you how I'd fix it in 15 mins, no cost. Sam

**Email after "send me info" (send within the hour):**
> Subject: [Business] website
>
> Hi [name], thanks for the chat. As promised: [one line about THE HOOK + screenshot].
> I'll put together a free homepage mockup for [Business] and a quick look at who's getting the [suburb] searches. Are you free [day] or [day] for 15 minutes to go through it?
> Sam, Locate Digital, [mobile]

**Follow-up cadence:** call, then text, then call 2 days later, then email, then a final call a week later. Five touches, then move on and log it.

---

## 8. After every call (10 seconds)

Fill in the CSV's last three columns:
- **Called?** Y
- **Outcome:** Booked / Send mockup / Call back / Not now / Not a fit / No answer
- **Follow-up:** date and time, plus one note in their words (e.g. "wants more hot water jobs, burned by previous agency")

That note is the opening line of your 15-minute meeting.
