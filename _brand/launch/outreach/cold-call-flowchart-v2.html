3. Objections

Agree, reframe, ask a question. Then go back to book it. Tap to open.

"What's the catch?"
"Fair question. There isn't one beyond the testimonial. I'm new to doing this with brokers, and the hardest thing to get when you're starting is proof. So I'd rather give a few firms something useful and earn the review. If it's not useful, you don't owe me anything, not even the review."
"We already use ChatGPT"
"Good, then you'll get even more out of it. Most teams use it like a search engine. The jump is setting it up with your own templates and tone, so the output's usable straight away. How are you using it now?"
"We're too busy"
"That's exactly why it's an hour. If it doesn't save you more than an hour in the first week, I've wasted your time. I can do before 9 or after 4."
"I'm worried about client privacy"
"You should be, and that's one of the most useful things we cover. A lot of brokers paste client details into free tools without knowing where the data goes. I'll show you which tasks are fine, what to keep out, and how business versions handle data differently."

Stay general. You're pointing out risks, not giving compliance advice.

"Send me some info"
"Happy to. Honestly, the info is the session itself. It's an hour and it's free. Tuesday or Thursday? If neither works, I'll email and you pick a time."
"Is this a sales pitch for something bigger?"
"I do run a paid workshop and AI policy for brokerages, so I won't pretend it doesn't exist. But I won't bring it up in the session. If you ask at the end, I'll tell you. If not, you've got free tricks and I've got a testimonial."

5. Running the hour

Say first: "Two rules today. We only use made-up or de-identified client details, and anything AI writes, a human checks before it goes anywhere."

0 to 10 minQuestions: tools, a normal day, the task they named, office rules on AI. Write their answers word for word.
10 to 45 minThree or four tricks on their real kind of task. Let them type, not you.
45 to 55 minPrivacy quick-check: fine tasks, what to keep out of free tools, why a business account matters.
55 to 60 minThe testimonial ask. Leave on time.
The five tricks to pick from
  1. Rough notes to file note in their format. Always check before saving.
  2. Missing-documents email from the checklist, in their tone.
  3. Plain-English explainer of a rate change or lender policy for a client.
  4. Saved instructions for their tone, sign-off and templates, so it's right first time.
  5. Marketing in five minutes: a de-identified settlement story into a Google Business post.
