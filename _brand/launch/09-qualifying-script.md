# 60-second qualifying script — calls & door-knocks

For your cold calls and door-knocks. The job here isn't to pitch. It's to find out **in 60 seconds whether this is a Gabby/Marco or a Sean** — so you don't burn a teardown (or an hour) on someone who can't fund a result.

Qualify first. Pitch second. See [07-client-personas.md] for who's who.

---

## What you're trying to learn (4 things)

You can read some off Google before you arrive, but confirm live:

1. **How many practitioners?** (3+ = good)
2. **How many locations?** (2+ = Marco)
3. **Are they advertising now, and who runs it?** (tells you mindset + budget)
4. **Is the owner the decision-maker, and are they in?** (no point pitching staff)

If 1 and 3 land well, they can likely fund a result. That's your green light.

---

## The door-knock opener

> "Hi, I'm Sam — I run a small marketing agency that works only with physio and osteo clinics around [area]. I'm not here to sell you anything today. Is [owner name] in? I had a quick look at your online setup and spotted a couple of things that might be costing you new patient bookings."

If the owner's not in:
> "No worries — who's the best person to talk to about getting more new patients in, and when's a good time to catch them?" *(Get a name + time. Leave the capability sheet.)*

---

## The cold-call opener

> "Hi [name], it's Sam from Locate Digital — I work only with physio and osteo clinics in Melbourne. Quick one, have I caught you at an okay time for 60 seconds? … I had a look at [Clinic]'s ads this morning and noticed a couple of things likely costing you new patient bookings. Before I waste your time though — can I ask you a couple of quick questions to see if it's even relevant?"

Then go to the qualifying questions. Asking permission to qualify makes it feel like *you're* screening *them*, which flips the dynamic.

---

## The qualifying questions (ask naturally, not like a form)

**1. "How many practitioners have you got at the moment?"**
- 3+ → good. Keep going.
- 1–2 → note it. Could still work if they're advertising and growing, but watch for Sean.

**2. "Just the one location, or a few?"**
- 2+ → Marco. Treat as a priority, pitch Scale later.

**3. "Are you running any ads at the moment — Google, Facebook? Who looks after that?"**
- "We've got an agency" → good (budget exists). Use the second-opinion angle.
- "I do a bit myself" → classic Gabby. Big opportunity.
- "Nothing, just word of mouth" → probe budget carefully before investing time.

**4. "What's the main thing you'd want more of — new patients, filling certain practitioners' books, a quiet day?"**
- Gets her talking about the pain in her words. Listen.

---

## The branch — what to do with the answer

**✅ Qualified (Gabby/Marco): 3+ practitioners, advertising or clearly can fund it.**
> "This is exactly the kind of clinic I help. Let me record you a free 3-minute teardown of what's running now and send it over — no charge, no pitch. What's the best email? And if it's useful, we grab 15 minutes after."

Book the next step. Log them in the tracker as qualified.

**⚠️ Borderline: 1–2 practitioners but advertising and growing.**
> "Got it. Let me send you a quick teardown anyway — and I run a free checklist for clinics at your stage that'll help straight away."

Send the [checklists](locatedigital.io/checklists). Keep warm. Only invest a full teardown if the budget signal is real.

**🚫 Sean: solo, no budget, can't fund spend, or already sounds needy.**
> "Honestly? At your stage, paid ads probably won't pay off yet — you'd likely spend more than the new patients are worth. The thing I'd do first is [one free tip / the checklist]. Come back to me once you've added a second practitioner and I'll happily take another look."

You lose nothing, you look honest (not desperate), and you've left the door open for when they grow. Don't book a teardown.

---

## How to spot Sean fast (the red flags)

- Asks for free work beyond the teardown on the first contact.
- Wants to "test it with $200" of ad spend.
- Lots of small unrelated requests ("can you also do my logo / posts / website").
- Hesitates or goes vague the moment budget comes up.
- Solo, brand new, no growth signal.

One of these is fine. Two or more, politely exit.

---

## The mindset

You're not begging for clients. You're a specialist screening for the right ones. The clinic that can't fund a result isn't a win you're missing — it's a bad result with your name on it that you're dodging. Qualify hard, and every teardown you record is aimed at someone who can actually become a paying Gabby.
