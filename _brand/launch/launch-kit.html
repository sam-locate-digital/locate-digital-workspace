01 — Positioning

Positioning & USP

Launch niche: physiotherapy & osteopathy clinics, Melbourne. Expansion path: broader allied health → dental, once 5–6 case studies are banked.

The one-sentence USP

We help Melbourne physio and osteo clinics book 20–40 new patients a month with Google and Meta ads, without wasting spend on no-shows or being locked into long contracts.

Use this everywhere: site, LinkedIn, email signature, outreach, calls.

Who you're for

  • Physio or osteo clinic, 1–3 practitioners, Melbourne metro
  • Doing $400k–$2M/yr
  • Owner is the practitioner — time-poor, not a marketer
  • Running ads badly, or relying only on word of mouth
  • Wants more new patients, doesn't want to learn ads

Who to say no to

  • Pre-revenue startups with no budget
  • Clients wanting to "test" $300/mo of ad spend
  • Anyone expecting a free brand build
  • Practitioners who won't commit 3 months
The promise behind it: you can defend this because you actually run ads well — most agencies can't. Lead with outcomes in their language (new patients, chair utilisation, ROAS). Never lead with deliverables.

03 — Outreach

Outreach

The early game is volume + personalisation + a free audit hook. Goal: 10 personalised approaches a day until you have 3 clients.

The hook

A free "3-minute ad teardown" — a short Loom where you screen-record their ads/site/Google listing and point out 2–3 things costing them new patients. Do the work first, then send it. Proves competence instead of claiming it.

Channels, in order

  1. Warm intros — anyone you know in or near allied health
  2. DM / email + free teardown
  3. Follow-up sequence — most replies land on follow-up 2 and 3

Scripts

Warm message
Hey [name], quick one — I've just started Locate Digital, helping Melbourne physio and osteo clinics get more new patients through Google and Meta ads. Not pitching you, but do you know any clinic owners who'd want a free 3-min review of their ads? Happy to just hand it over.
Cold email — subject: Found 3 things costing [Clinic] new patients
Hi [name],

I had a look at [Clinic]'s ads and Google listing this morning and recorded a quick 3-minute video — spotted a few things likely costing you new patient bookings (one's an easy fix).

No pitch, no catch. Here's the video: [Loom link]

I run Locate Digital — we help Melbourne physio and osteo clinics book more new patients through Google and Meta. If any of it's useful, happy to chat. If not, keep the video.

Cheers, Sam — Locate Digital
Instagram DM
Hi [name] — I help Melbourne physio/osteo clinics get more new patients through ads. Recorded a free 3-min teardown of [Clinic]'s current ads, spotted a couple of quick wins. Want me to send it over? No pitch.
Follow-up 1 (day 3)
Hi [name], did the teardown video come through ok? Happy to walk you through the fixes on a quick call if useful.
Follow-up 2 (day 7)
Hey [name] — last one from me. The biggest thing I saw was [specific issue]. Even if we never work together, worth fixing. Want me to send the how-to?
Follow-up 3 (day 14)
Hi [name], I'll leave it here. If new patient numbers ever become a focus, I'm one message away. All the best with the clinic.

On the call

  1. Ask their goals + current new-patient numbers before pitching
  2. Walk through what you found in the teardown
  3. Recommend Growth; frame price against a patient's lifetime value ($400–1,500+)
  4. Close on the $750 audit or a 3-month Growth retainer + $1,500 setup
Daily rhythm until 3 clients: 10 new prospects added · 3–5 teardown videos sent · all due follow-ups sent · tracking sheet updated. First 1–2 clients get a discounted rate in exchange for a case study + testimonial — trade price for proof, never work free.

05 — Execution

90-day launch plan

Goal: 3 paying clients and ~$8–10k/mo recurring by day 90.

Weeks 1–2 — Foundation

  • Finalise USP sentence
  • Build one-page site + Calendly link
  • Rewrite LinkedIn + Instagram bios around the USP
  • Set up business email, CRM/sheet, invoicing
  • Turn MOSIC work into your first case study
  • Build the prospect tracking sheet

Weeks 2–4 — First outreach wave

  • List first 50 Melbourne physio/osteo clinics
  • Message everyone warm you already know
  • Record + send 3–5 teardown videos a day
  • Run the follow-up sequence on every prospect
  • Target: first booked call by week 3, first client by week 4

Weeks 4–8 — Land first clients

  • Reach 100 prospects
  • Hold the daily rhythm
  • Offer first 1–2 clients a discounted rate for a case study
  • Onboard properly: setup fee, tracking, campaign build
  • Target: 2 clients signed

Weeks 8–12 — Prove and tighten

  • Deliver results, track new-patient numbers weekly
  • Get first testimonial + case study written up
  • Sign client 3 at full rate
  • Ask every happy client for one referral
  • Start running your own small campaign to the niche
The one metric that matters now: teardown videos sent per week. Calls, clients, and revenue are all a function of outreach volume — protect this number above all else.
