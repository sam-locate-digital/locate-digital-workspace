The automations that actually move the needle

Not automation for its own sake. These five remove the most hours.

1. Booking → onboarding. Calendly booking fires Zapier/n8n → creates the CRM deal, pings Slack, and on signup spins up the Drive folder + runs the Claude client-onboarding skill. Saves ~1 hr per client.
2. Monthly reporting. n8n pulls Google Ads + GA4 data on a schedule → Looker Studio dashboard + Claude monthly-report-narrative writes the insights. Turns a half-day per client into minutes.
3. Content engine. Your reels-script, social-post, blog and GMB skills run on a content calendar → near-zero-cost daily presence for both Locate and clients.
4. Inbound triage. n8n + Claude scores inbound leads and drafts the first reply, so nothing sits cold while you're in calls.
5. Account anomaly alerts. A scheduled check on client ad accounts flags spend spikes or conversion drops before the client notices. Retention gold.
My honest pick on Zapier vs n8n: start on Zapier or Make in months 2–3 — speed matters more than cost when you've got 3 clients. Switch to n8n around month 4–5 once you're running real volume: it's self-hosted (or cheap cloud), has no per-task pricing, and its AI nodes plug straight into Claude. Don't start on n8n — the setup overhead isn't worth it until you've proven the workflows.
