# Client personas — Locate Digital

Built from Sam's own targeting rules (2026-06-27), not guesswork. Two things drive these:

1. **The floor rises with proof.** Early on, take clients who can fund real ad spend and respect the scope — Starter is fine for your first few. Once you've got 3 clients + case studies, raise the floor to Growth ($3,500/mo+) and stay there.
2. **Avoid clients who can't fund a result.** The problem isn't a low price, it's a clinic that can't put enough into ad spend to actually book patients — that churns and hands you a weak result. Avoid those at any price.

So you've got **two buyers you chase** (Gabby, Marco) and **one anti-persona you decline** (Sean) — but the *price floor* is a phase, not a permanent rule.

> Honest note: you have no signed clients yet, so these are a starting hypothesis based on your niche and rules. Re-sharpen them after your first 3–5 clients with what's actually true.

### The price floor is a phase, not a day-one rule

| Phase | Floor | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Now → first 3 clients** | Take Starter ($1,800) or discounted, *if* they fund real spend + hold scope | Cash + case studies + testimonials matter more than purity when the calendar's empty |
| **After 3 clients + proof** | Raise the floor to Growth+ | Now you can afford to be picky, and proof justifies the price |

## The revenue filter — qualify BEFORE you pitch

This is the most useful thing right now. You're cold-calling and door-knocking, so you need to disqualify fast.

A Growth client pays you ~$3,500/mo management **plus** ad spend (call it $3k–5k/mo to hit 20–40 patients). That's roughly **$7k–8k/mo, ~$90k/yr, in marketing.** Healthy clinics spend 7–10% of revenue on marketing. So:

| Can they fund a result? | Clinic revenue | Likely tier |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Can't fund real ad spend — decline or refer | under ~$400k/yr | none — they'll churn |
| ✅ OK early, if scoped tight | ~$400k–$800k/yr | Starter $1,800 (first clients only) |
| ✅ Growth, your sweet spot | ~$800k–$1.5M/yr | Growth $3,500 |
| ✅ Scale, your dream | $1.5M+/yr | Scale $6,500+ |

**Fast proxies for revenue** (you can read these off Google before you call): number of practitioners (3+ is a good sign), multiple locations, fully-booked online calendar, already advertising, a real website not a one-pager. Rule of thumb: **3+ practitioners or 2+ locations = worth a conversation.**

## 1. Growth-ready Gabby — PRIMARY target (Growth, $3,500/mo)

> "I want more leads and more bookings. I don't have time to run the ads myself, and I'd rather someone who actually knows what they're doing handle it."

**Snapshot**
- Established physio or osteo clinic, **3–4 practitioners, one location**, Melbourne metro.
- **~$800k–$1.5M/yr** — enough to fund Growth-level marketing without flinching.
- Owner is the decision-maker. Still treats patients, runs the business around it.

**What she's doing now**
- Either has an agency she's lukewarm on, or runs **basic ads herself off the back of a few YouTube videos** — no real strategy or tracking.
- Knows enough to know it's not working as well as it should.

**What she wants** (in her words)
- More leads. More bookings. Predictable, not feast-or-famine.

**Why she's a good fit**
- Revenue supports your fee. Owner can say yes without a committee. Already believes in marketing, just wants it done right.

**Objections you'll actually hear** (your answers #8)
- **"We've already got an agency."** → "Happy to do a free teardown of what they're running. If it's solid, I'll tell you. If it's leaking money, you'll want to know." Compete on tracking + specialisation.
- **"Last agency burned us."** → No lock-in, 3-month proof, real numbers (patients booked + cost per patient), and you run the ads yourself.

**Where you'll reach her** (your answers #9)
- Cold call / door-knock off Google Maps, plus the free teardown as the door-opener. Later: referrals once you have proof.

**Message that lands**
- "More new patients booked, fully tracked, no lock-in. Run by a physio/osteo specialist who does the work himself."

## 2. Multi-site Marco — SECONDARY target / dream client (Scale, $6,500/mo+)

> "I don't want someone to just 'run some ads'. I want a partner who treats this like a business and shows me the return."

**Snapshot**
- **2–3 locations, or one large clinic with 5+ practitioners**, sometimes mixed allied health.
- **$1.5M+/yr.** Has admin or a practice manager, but **still the decision-maker.**
- More business owner than clinician now.

**What he's doing now**
- Already spends on marketing. Has an agency he's not thrilled with, or a messy patchwork. Wants one accountable partner.

**What he wants**
- Scale new-patient flow across sites, fill a new location, clear ROI.

**Why he's the dream**
- Highest fee, lowest neediness per dollar, and a multi-location case study is gold for your next pitches.

**Objections**
- **"What makes you different from my last agency?"** → specialist focus, you run it yourself, outcome reporting not vanity reports.
- **"Can you handle multiple locations?"** → full-funnel, landing pages per site, fortnightly calls.

**Where you'll reach him**
- Referrals from accountants/consultants, LinkedIn, industry events. Wants a proper proposal + case study, not a cold DM.

**Message that lands**
- "A specialist growth partner for multi-location allied health. Fully tracked, senior attention, ROI you can take to your accountant."

## 3. Anti-persona: Shoestring Sean — DECLINE or refer (your answer #3)

> "Can you just do a quick thing for me? Oh, and can you also change this, and post this, and what about that…"

**Snapshot**
- Solo or brand-new clinic, **under ~$400k/yr**, single chair, outer suburb or just opened.
- Wants results but **can't fund enough ad spend to get one.**

**Why you say no — it's not the price, it's the result**
- **Can't fund a result.** $500–800/mo of ad spend won't book 20–40 patients. The campaign underperforms, they churn, and you've got a weak result with your name on it.
- **Needy.** Lots of small requests that blow past the hours (your answer #3).
- **Slows the path to $100k.** Your time is better spent landing one Gabby than servicing three Seans.

> Note the difference: a disciplined **Starter** client at $1,800 who *can* fund spend and holds the scope is fine, especially early. Sean is the one who can't fund a result at any price. Don't confuse the two.

**How to say no well**
- "Honestly, paid ads won't pay off at your stage yet — you'd be spending more than the new patients are worth. Come back when you've added a second practitioner." Refer them to your free checklists. Keeps the relationship warm for later.

**Early-stage reality**
- Your **first 2–3 clients** may be smaller than Gabby, and that's the right call — trade a discount for proof and testimonials. The only hard line even when you're hungry: they must be able to **fund enough ad spend to actually get a result.** A cheap client with a good result is a case study; a cheap client with a bad result is a liability.

## How to use these

- **90% of your effort goes to Gabby.** Homepage, main LP, most ads and outreach speak to her.
- **Marco is the upside.** Pitch Scale when the revenue signals (locations, practitioner count) are there.
- **Sean is a filter, not a target.** Use the revenue table to disqualify on the call so you don't waste a teardown.
- **Qualify before you pitch.** Practitioner count and locations off Google tell you the tier before you even dial.
- **Revisit after 5–6 clients.** Replace these hypotheses with what your real clients tell you.
