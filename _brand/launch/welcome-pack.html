What we'll measure

  • New patients booked — the only number that really matters.
  • Cost per booking — what each new patient costs in ad spend.
  • Spend vs return — every dollar tracked, no vanity metrics.

Give the campaigns the first 4–6 weeks to gather data and settle. Paid media needs a little runway before it hits full stride — that's why we run a 3-month minimum.

A couple of house rules

  • Sign off creative quickly — the faster you approve, the faster we launch.
  • Tell us when something changes — a new service, a price change, a fully booked week. It all affects the ads.
  • Send us the wins — when a patient says "I found you on Google", let us know. It sharpens everything.
