# Positioning — Locate Digital

Launch niche: **physiotherapy & osteopathy clinics, Melbourne.**
Expansion path: broader allied health (chiro, podiatry, myo) → dental, once 5–6 case studies are banked.

## The one-sentence USP

> We help Melbourne physio and osteo clinics book 20–40 new patients a month with Google and Meta ads, without wasting spend on no-shows or being locked into long contracts.

Use this everywhere: site, LinkedIn, email signature, outreach, calls.

## Why this works

It names a specific person (Melbourne physio/osteo owner), a specific outcome they actually care about (new patients booked, not "leads" or "engagement"), and removes their two biggest fears (wasted spend, lock-in contracts).

## Who you're for (ideal client profile)

- Physio or osteo clinic, 1–3 practitioners, Melbourne metro
- Doing $400k–$2M/yr
- Owner is the practitioner — time-poor, not a marketer
- Already running some ads (badly) or relying only on word of mouth
- Wants more new patients but doesn't want to learn ads themselves

## Who you're NOT for (say no to these)

- Pre-revenue startups with no budget
- Clients who want to "test" $300/mo of ad spend
- Anyone who wants you to also build their brand from scratch for free
- Practitioners who won't commit 3 months

## The promise behind the USP

You can defend this because you actually run the ads well (most agencies can't). Lead with outcomes in their language: new patients, chair/table utilisation, return on ad spend. Never lead with deliverables (posts, impressions, reach).

## Proof assets to build first

1. MOSIC osteopathy work → turn into your first case study
2. Before/after new-patient numbers from your first 1–2 clients
3. A simple "here's what we found wrong in your account" audit you can show
