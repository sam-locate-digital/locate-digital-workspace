# Gabby outreach — cold email & follow-ups

Persona-tuned outreach for **Growth-ready Gabby** (see [07-client-personas.md]). This sharpens the generic scripts in [03-outreach.md] — use these when the prospect is a qualified Gabby (3+ practitioners, can fund real spend).

**Why your old approach got nothing:** you emailed "osteos, physios, service businesses and allied health" with one generic message. It read like every other agency. Gabby gets that spam weekly and deletes it. The fix: narrow to qualified clinics, lead with proof (a teardown you've already done), and write to *her specific* situation.

**The golden rule:** do the work first. Record the 3-minute teardown, *then* reach out. The teardown is the hook, not the pitch.

---

## Step 1 — Record the teardown first (before any message)

Before you contact a qualified clinic, spend 3 minutes recording a Loom where you screen-share and show 2–3 things costing them new patients. For Gabby, the gold is almost always:

- **No conversion tracking** — "you can't see what a booking actually costs you."
- **Wasted spend** — ads showing outside her catchment, or broad match burning budget.
- **A weak landing experience** — clicks going to a homepage, not a booking-focused page.

Name the clinic out loud, be specific, be generous. End with: "happy to walk you through fixing these, no pitch."

---

## Step 2 — The cold email (Gabby version)

**Subject lines (test these):**
- Found 3 things costing [Clinic] new patient bookings
- Quick teardown of [Clinic]'s Google ads (3 min)
- [Clinic] — your ads are leaking, here's where

**Body:**

> Hi [Owner first name],
>
> I had a look at [Clinic]'s ads and Google listing this morning and recorded a quick 3-minute video — there are a couple of things that are likely costing you new patient bookings. One's a 5-minute fix.
>
> The main one: [specific finding, e.g. "you're paying for clicks from people outside [suburb], and there's no tracking set up so you can't see what a booking actually costs you."]
>
> No pitch, no catch — here's the video: [Loom link]
>
> I'm Sam. I run Locate Digital, and I only work with Melbourne physio and osteo clinics — getting them more new patients through Google and Meta, fully tracked, no lock-in. If any of it's useful, happy to chat. If not, keep the video.
>
> Cheers,
> Sam
> Locate Digital · [phone] · locatedigital.io

**Why it works for Gabby:** specific finding (not generic), proof before pitch, names her exact niche (signals you're a specialist not a generalist), and the two things she fears — wasted spend and not knowing what works — are right there.

## Step 3 — Follow-ups (where most replies actually come from)

Don't quit after one email. Replies cluster on follow-up 2 and 3.

**Follow-up 1 — day 3:**
> Hi [name], did the teardown video land okay? Happy to walk you through the fixes on a quick 10-min call if it's useful.

**Follow-up 2 — day 7 (lead with the single biggest finding):**
> Hey [name], last useful one from me. The biggest thing I saw was [specific issue, e.g. "no conversion tracking, so you're flying blind on what each new patient costs"]. Even if we never work together, it's worth fixing — want me to send the how-to?

**Follow-up 3 — day 14 (the break-up):**
> Hi [name], I'll leave it here. If getting more new patients in the door ever becomes a focus, I'm one message away. All the best with the clinic.

> The break-up email often gets the reply. Removing the pressure makes people respond.

## Handling Gabby's two real objections

These are the ones you've actually hit (persona answer #8).

**"We've already got an agency."**
> Totally fair. Most clinics I talk to do. Tell you what — let me do a free teardown of what they're running. If it's solid, I'll tell you straight and you've lost nothing. If it's leaking money, you'll want to know. Either way you get a free second opinion.

*Why it works:* you don't trash the incumbent, you offer a no-risk second opinion, and your tracking/specialisation usually exposes gaps a generalist agency left.

**"We got burned by an agency before."**
> I hear that a lot, and it's usually the same story — locked into a contract, vanity reports, no real accountability. That's exactly why I run no lock-in, a 3-month proof window, and I report on one thing: new patients booked and what each one cost. If I'm not delivering, you walk.

*Why it works:* you name her exact bad experience, then remove each part of it.
