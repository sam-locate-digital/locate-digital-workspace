The situation

MOSIC is an established Melbourne osteopathy clinic with a strong patient base and eight practitioners across two locations. They were running ~870 appointments a month and bringing in around 48 new patients a month, largely through reputation and existing patients.

With eight practitioners and two locations, there was capacity to take on more new patients — but no accountable, optimised acquisition channel feeding the diary.

What we did

  • Rebuilt Google Ads around high-intent local searches across both catchments, cutting spend wasted on out-of-area clicks.
  • Set up conversion tracking tying ad clicks through to actual Cliniko bookings.
  • Built service-specific landing pages (scoliosis, golf osteo) to match search intent.
  • Monthly reporting consolidating Google Ads, SEO traffic and booking outcomes.
