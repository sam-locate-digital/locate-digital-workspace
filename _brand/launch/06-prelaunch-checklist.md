## THE RESIGNATION GATE — don't quit until ALL of these are true

This is the most important section. Working full time gives you a safety net to build and sell with zero financial pressure. Use it. Don't hand in your notice on hope.

- ⬜ **2 paying clients signed** (or 1 signed + 2 in active proposal stage). Proof the model works with real money.
- ⬜ **~$5k/mo recurring locked in.** Half your 90-day target, already banked, before you lose the salary.
- ⬜ **3–6 months personal living expenses saved** in a separate buffer. Covers the gap while you scale from 2 clients to a full book.
- ⬜ **Business legally trading** — ABN active, bank account open, insured, contract ready to sign.
- ⬜ **Delivery proven once** — you've run MOSIC (or client 1) end to end and can repeat it without scrambling.

If those five are true in September, resign. If they're not, the side-hustle runway is doing its job — keep going.

## PHASE A — Legal & money foundations (start NOW, processing takes weeks)

These have lead times (ASIC, bank, insurer underwriting). Start week one so they're not blocking you in September.

### Business structure & registration
- ⬜ **Decide structure: Sole trader vs Pty Ltd company.** Start as **sole trader** (cheap, simple, register today) unless you want liability protection / plan to hire fast — then Pty Ltd. You can convert later. *Decision needed from you.*
- ⬜ **Get an ABN** — free, ~15 min at abr.gov.au. Non-negotiable, everything else hangs off it.
- ⬜ **Register the business name "Locate Digital"** with ASIC (~$44 for 1yr / $102 for 3yr) — unless trading under your own name. Check name availability first.
- ⬜ **GST registration** — only legally required at $75k turnover, but register early so you can claim GST back on tools/setup and look established to clients. *Recommend registering now.*
- ⬜ **Domain + entity name match** — confirm locatedigital.io ✅ aligns with the registered name.

### Banking & accounting
- ⬜ **Open a business bank account** (or at minimum a separate account if sole trader). Never mix personal and business money.
- ⬜ **Set up accounting software — Xero** (~$35/mo, AU standard, accountant-friendly). Alternative: MYOB.
- ⬜ **Engage an accountant / bookkeeper** for setup, BAS, and EOFY. Even a one-off setup call is worth it. Ask about PAYG instalments and putting tax aside.
- ⬜ **Payment processing:**
  - ⬜ **Stripe** for card payments + invoicing.
  - ⬜ **GoCardless or Stripe direct debit** for retainers — auto-debit monthly so you're not chasing invoices. Critical for recurring revenue.
- ⬜ **Invoice template** with ABN, GST line, payment terms (7 days), bank + Stripe details.

### Insurance
- ⬜ **Professional Indemnity insurance** — covers you if your advice/work causes a client a loss. Clients (esp. bigger ones) will ask. ~$50–100/mo.
- ⬜ **Public Liability** — often bundled with PI. Get a combined small-business package (BizCover, Aon, etc. for quotes).

### Contracts & policy
- ⬜ **Client service agreement / retainer contract** — scope, deliverables, 3-month min term, payment terms, ad-spend-is-separate clause, IP, termination, no guarantee of specific results. Get a lawyer or a vetted AU template (Sprintlaw, LawPath) — do NOT freehand this.
- ⬜ **Privacy Policy + Terms** on the website (you handle client ad accounts + data — required, esp. with Meta/Google data access).
- ⬜ **Master Services Agreement vs per-project** — decide your default. Recommend MSA + per-client scope.

## PHASE G — Personal & financial runway

The stuff that protects YOU through the transition.

- ⬜ **Calculate your number** — minimum monthly personal expenses. That's the MRR you must beat to replace the salary.
- ⬜ **Savings buffer** — 3–6 months expenses set aside before resigning (see the Gate).
- ⬜ **Check your employment contract** — non-compete / non-solicit / moonlighting clauses. Make sure building this on the side doesn't breach it. *Check this early.*
- ⬜ **Resignation plan** — notice period, finish date, handover, and don't burn the bridge (ex-colleagues are referrals).
- ⬜ **Tax set-aside habit** — park ~25–30% of every payment for tax/GST from day one.
- ⬜ **Time-block the side hustle** — fixed hours each week for outreach while employed. The one metric (teardowns sent/week) needs protecting now, not in September.

**Weeks 9–10:** Second client. Buffer topped up. Deliver client 1 end to end. Hit the Gate → resign.
