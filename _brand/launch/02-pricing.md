# Pricing — Locate Digital

All AUD, ex GST, monthly unless noted. Management fee only — client ad spend is paid by them directly to the platform and is separate.

## Retainer tiers

| Tier | Price/mo | For | Includes | Your hours/mo |
|------|----------|-----|----------|---------------|
| **Starter** | $1,800 | Solo physio/osteo, 1 location | 1 channel (Meta or Google), monthly report, 1 call/mo | ~10 |
| **Growth** | $3,500 | Most clinics, 2–3 practitioners | Meta + Google, GA4/GTM tracking, monthly report + call, creative refresh | ~20 |
| **Scale** | $6,500+ | Multi-location / higher spend | Full-funnel, both channels, CRO, landing pages, fortnightly calls, priority | ~35 |

Anchor every pitch on **Growth**. Most clinics belong there.

Rule of thumb: charge the higher of the flat retainer or **15–20% of monthly ad spend.** A clinic spending $8k/mo on ads belongs on Growth at minimum.

## One-off / project work

- Paid ad + tracking audit: **$750** (tripwire — credit it back if they sign a retainer)
- Landing page build: **$1,800–3,500**
- GA4 + GTM / conversion tracking setup: **$1,500–2,500**
- New patient campaign build (one-off): **$2,000–3,000**

## Tradie website package

Built from the tradie demo template (`_brand/Website/demos/tradie`, live at demo.locatedigital.io/tradie). ~12–15 hours per build.

| Option | Upfront | Monthly | Notes |
|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Website** | **$5,999** | $149 care plan | Two trades, up to 16 suburb pages, 4 service pages |
| Website, smaller | $4,500 | $149 care plan | One trade, up to 8 suburb pages, 2 service pages |
| Website, bigger | $7,000+ (quoted) | $149 care plan | More services, extra suburbs, custom design |
| **Website + Google (recommended)** | **$4,499** | **$1,800** | Website at $2,999 + the $1,500 setup fee. Monthly fee is ongoing paid media management, hosting included. 3-month minimum |
| Website, paid monthly | $1,999 | $399 for 12 months + $149 care plan | Year one totals $8,575 vs $7,787 paying upfront with the care plan |

- **Anchor on Website + Google.** "$5,999 on its own, or $4,499 with Google" makes the bundle the obvious pick. The retainer is where the money is.
- **Why $5,999:** priced as separate pieces from this rate card it comes to ~$7,400 (tracking $1,500, 16 suburb pages $2,400, 4 service pages $1,000, core 7-page site ~$2,500), so $5,999 is an honest ~20% package saving.
- **Included:** copy from a 45-minute interview, their real reviews/licences/photos, booking form to email with photo uploads, mobile call bar, GA4 + call and booking tracking, linked Google Business Profile, live on their domain in 10 working days, 2 rounds of changes.
- **Care plan ($149/mo):** hosting, security updates, up to 1 hour of small edits a month, short monthly calls-and-bookings email. Declined? Charge ~$300 one-off to hand the site over to their own hosting.
- **Add-ons:** extra service page $250, extra suburb page $150. Logo, photography, blog and automatic SMS confirmations quoted separately.
- **Ads landing pages:** send ads to the matching service or suburb page first. A stripped-back landing page (emergency, an offer, or a high-spend ad group) is an add-on from month 2–3, ~$600–900 when built from their existing site.
- **Payment:** 50% of the build upfront, 50% at launch. The client owns their domain, content and accounts.
- **Launch rate:** first 2–3 tradies get the website at ~$3,500 in exchange for a testimonial and before/after numbers. Then hold full price.

## Setup fee

**$1,500 one-off onboarding** on any new retainer. Covers audit, pixel/conversion setup, account build, and the heavy first-month lift. Non-negotiable — it stops you bleeding hours before revenue lands.

## Rules

1. Minimum **3-month engagement** on retainers. Paid media needs runway.
2. Ad spend is **separate and paid by the client.** Say it out loud on every call.
3. Starter clients get **1 call/mo, full stop.** Hold the line or they eat Growth-level hours.
4. Raise prices as proof stacks. Your first 2 clients pay launch rates; client 5 pays more.

## Effective rate target

$150–250/hr. If a client repeatedly blows past their hour band, that's the signal to move them up a tier, not to absorb the hours.
