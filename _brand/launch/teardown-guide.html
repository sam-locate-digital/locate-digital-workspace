The teardown guide

Your single highest-leverage outreach tool. A 3-minute video that proves you know your stuff before the prospect pays a cent.

What a teardown is

A short screen-recorded video (use Loom) where you pull up a prospect's current marketing and point out 2–3 specific things costing them new patients. You do the work first, then send it. No pitch.

Why it works

  • It skips the "trust me" problem. Every new agency has it. A teardown replaces a claim with proof.
  • It's hard to ignore. A generic cold email gets deleted. "I already looked at your account, here's what's wrong" gets watched.
  • It filters. People who watch and reply are genuinely interested. Tyre-kickers ignore it and save you time.

How to record one

1
Pick a clinic from your tracker.
2
Open their stuff on screen: their ads (check the Meta Ad Library + a Google search to see what's running), their website, and their Google Business listing.
3
Hit record on Loom. Talk through 2–3 specific problems, plain and direct. Lead with the biggest one.
4
End with one clear next step: "Happy to walk you through the fixes on a quick call."
5
Send it with a short message (scripts below).

The 3-minute script

0:00 – 0:20 · Open
"Hi [name], Sam from Locate Digital. I had a quick look at how [Clinic] is showing up online this morning and recorded a few things I noticed — should be useful whether we ever speak or not."
0:20 – 2:30 · The 2–3 findings (biggest first)
Walk through, on screen, things like:
• "You're running ads but sending clicks to your homepage, not a dedicated page — that leaks bookings."
• "Your Google listing has no booking link, so people have to hunt for how to contact you."
• "You're targeting all of Melbourne, so you're paying for clicks from people 40 minutes away who'll never come in."

For each: name it, show it, say what it's costing, give the fix.
2:30 – 3:00 · Close
"None of this is hard to fix. If you want, I'll walk you through it on a quick call — no obligation. Either way, hope it helps."

The send messages

No pitch, no catch: [Loom link]. I run Locate Digital — we help Melbourne physio and osteo clinics book more new patients. If any of it's useful, happy to chat. If not, keep the video. Cheers, Sam.
Instagram DM
Hi [name] — I help Melbourne physio/osteo clinics get more new patients through ads. Recorded a free 3-min teardown of [Clinic]'s current ads, spotted a couple of quick wins. Want me to send it over? No pitch.

Rules

3 minutes max. Longer and they won't watch.
Lead with the biggest problem. Don't bury it under small stuff.
Be helpful, not salesy. Give the fix away. The pitch is implied: "if you want this done properly, that's what I do."
One clear next step. A call. Not three options.
The number that matters: teardowns sent per week. Calls, clients and revenue are all downstream of it. Protect this above everything else — aim for 3–5 a day until you have 3 clients.
