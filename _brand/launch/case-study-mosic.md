## The situation

MOSIC is an established Melbourne osteopathy clinic with a strong patient base and eight practitioners across two locations. They were running ~870 appointments a month and bringing in around **48 new patients a month** (April 2026 baseline), largely through reputation and existing patients.

The opportunity: with eight practitioners and two locations, there was capacity to take on more new patients, but no accountable, optimised acquisition channel feeding the diary.

---

## What we did

- **Rebuilt Google Ads** around high-intent local searches across both catchments (CBD and Essendon), cutting spend wasted on clicks outside their service area.
- **Set up proper conversion tracking** tying ad clicks through to actual Cliniko bookings, so every dollar of spend could be measured against new patients booked.
- **Built targeted landing pages** for key services (e.g. scoliosis treatment, golf-specific osteo) to match search intent and lift conversion.
- **Monthly reporting** consolidating Google Ads, SEO traffic, and booking outcomes into a single plain-English report.
