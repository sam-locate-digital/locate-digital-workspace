# 90-day launch plan — Locate Digital

Goal: 3 paying clients and ~$8–10k/mo recurring by end of day 90. That's the foundation everything else scales from.

## Weeks 1–2 — Foundation

- [ ] Finalise USP sentence (see [01-positioning.md])
- [ ] Build one-page site from [04-website-copy.md] + Calendly booking link
- [ ] Rewrite LinkedIn + Instagram bios around the USP
- [ ] Set up business email, simple CRM/sheet, invoicing
- [ ] Turn MOSIC work into your first case study (numbers + before/after)
- [ ] Build the prospect tracking sheet (name, owner, contact, ads y/n, stage, next action)

## Weeks 2–4 — First outreach wave

- [ ] List first 50 Melbourne physio/osteo clinics
- [ ] Message everyone warm you already know
- [ ] Record + send 3–5 teardown videos a day
- [ ] Run the follow-up sequence on every prospect
- [ ] Target: first booked call by end of week 3, first client by end of week 4

## Weeks 4–8 — Land first clients

- [ ] Reach 100 prospects on the list
- [ ] Keep daily rhythm: 10 new prospects, 3–5 teardowns, all follow-ups
- [ ] Offer first 1–2 clients a discounted rate for a case study + testimonial
- [ ] Onboard properly: setup fee, tracking, campaign build
- [ ] Target: 2 clients signed

## Weeks 8–12 — Prove and tighten

- [ ] Deliver results, collect new-patient numbers weekly
- [ ] Get first testimonial + case study written up
- [ ] Sign client 3 at full rate
- [ ] Ask every happy client for one referral
- [ ] Start running your own small Meta/Google campaign to your niche
- [ ] Target: 3 clients, ~$8–10k/mo recurring

## Daily non-negotiables (until 3 clients)

1. 10 new prospects added
2. 3–5 teardown videos sent
3. All due follow-ups sent
4. Tracking sheet updated

## What comes after 90 days

Once you've got 3 clients and proof, the game shifts from outreach to systems: productise delivery, raise prices, lean on referrals, then make your first hire to take delivery off your plate. See the scaling path discussed for the road to $100k/mo.

## The one metric that matters now

**Teardown videos sent per week.** Everything downstream — calls, clients, revenue — is a function of outreach volume. Protect this number above all else.
