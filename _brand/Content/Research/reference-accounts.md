# Reference accounts

The five accounts Sam picked to model, studied on 2026-11-04 from each account's latest 10–12 reels (via vidiQ). Model the patterns, never copy lines, footage or graphics. Full reel breakdowns are in `swipe-file.md` in this folder.

## @aiwithremy: AI tools for business, series-led
- Biggest reels: "I banned AI slop at my company" (744K plays, 13.9K comments), "500 hours of short form in one Claude skill" (319K, 23.7K comments). Both are **Skill of the Week** episodes.
- **Series**: branded title card ("SKILL of the week") in the opening seconds, same intro line every week: "Welcome to Skill of the Week, where every week I share one useful agent skill."
- **Credibility without client results**: "500 hours studying", a graphic of 12 tools tested with 11 marked as slop, "I've made it mandatory for my whole team", real before/after screens.
- **CTA**: comment "SKILL" at the end, with a pattern interrupt (standing on the couch).
- **Short reels**: "had to take a walk while Opus works at home", 5 s, 59K plays. A list overlay on walking footage.
- Model: the series, the testing-as-proof, the polished UI graphics.

## @tahlia.kennedy: Sydney founder, journey and marketing opinion
- Biggest reels: "today was my last day at kinso" (9 s, 473K), "I quit my dream job" (40 s, 183K), "one shot at this life" (10 s, 80K), "the biggest art in marketing right now" (47 s, 75K), "It's time I reintroduce myself" (61 s, 68K).

- **Journey content is short and real**: milestone moments filmed as they happen, one line of text.

- Model: short milestone reels, story structure, the reintroduction reel, posting two versions.

## @cindiezhu: AI "codes" and prompts
- Biggest reels: a 10 s AI photo reel (12.6M), "Top 4 ChatGPT image codes" (1.5M, 25.5K comments).
- Every reel: comment keyword ("GPT", "study", "MANGA") for 48–100 more prompts.
- Posts 4–5 variants of one idea in a day with different first lines.
- Format: title card ("5 ChatGPT Codes to learn 10x faster") plus phone screen recordings. Cheap to make.
- Model: the copy-ready prompt as the value, prompt carousels, keyword-for-more CTAs.

## @minolee.mp4: content and Claude workflows, building in public

- **Series**: "Day 2 of road to $1M this month", with a progress bar on screen.
- Many short text-led reels with numbered tips in the caption.
- Rules from the 7-ways reel: plain 2–3 word captions, real screenshots not icons, cut all dead space.
- Model: the build-in-public series, captions that carry the list.

## @jun_yuh: storytelling
- Biggest reels: a family story (33M), a storytelling masterclass (3.9M), "everyday / once a week / once a month" grid (10 s, 322K).
- **Testing an intro reel**: six versions of "if you don't know me yet, I'm Jun…" in two days.
- Comment keyword for a challenge waitlist ("STORYTELLING", 10.7K on the waitlist).
- Model: the intro reel, the habit grid, story-first openings.

## Patterns across all five
1. Test hooks by posting variants (Trial Reels). One idea, several first lines.
2. Short text-over-footage reels (5–10 s) reach as far as polished teaching reels.
3. A named, recurring series gives people a reason to follow.
4. A pinned intro reel converts profile visitors.
5. Credibility comes from visible work (tests, screens, hours, own use), not client results.
6. Teaching reels use comment keywords; journey reels use "follow along".
