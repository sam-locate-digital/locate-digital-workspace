# Reel: Visitors but no calls

**Format:** value list, ~55s
**Post:** 7–9pm AEDT

## Hook
- **Title card:** Visitors but no calls? Check these 4
- **Spoken hook:** "Why is your website getting visitors but your phone isn't ringing, even after you paid for a new site?"
- **Frame-1 visual:** split screen. Top: a website analytics card with visitors going up and a calls card flat at the same time (dummy numbers, or real ones with sign-off). Bottom: Sam to camera.
- **Formula:** Failed-fix question. Inspo: [Content Principles "why is AI so bad" reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeeroIoRpnr/)

## Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Why is your website getting visitors but your phone isn't ringing, even after you paid for a new site? | Visitors but no **calls**? | Split screen: visitors climbing, calls flat |
| 4–9s | Because a new site fixes how it looks. Nobody fixed what happens after someone lands on it. | After they **land** | Wide, punch in on "lands on it" |
| 9–18s | One. Your number isn't tappable on mobile. Most of your visitors are on a phone. Make it one tap to call, at the top of every page. | 1 One tap to **call** | Framework step 1; Sam's thumb tapping a number on a real mobile site |
| 18–26s | Two. Your form asks too much. Name, number, what you need. That's it. Every extra box is another reason to leave. | 2 Three **boxes** max | Screen recording: a 9-field form shrinking to 3 |
| 26–34s | Three. There's no reason to call today. "Contact us" isn't a reason. "Same-day quotes across Adelaide" is. | 3 Give a reason **today** | Framework step 3; before/after button text |
| 34–43s | Four. Nothing's tracked. If you can't see which page or ad your calls come from, you're guessing which bit is broken. Set up call and form tracking in GA4 before you change anything else. | 4 Track it **first** | Screen recording: GA4 key events list, details blurred |
| 43–50s | **[Proof]** [Sam: real client result, e.g. "We made these four changes for a [client type]. Enquiries went from [real number]% of visitors to [real number]%."] | [Sam: real number]% **enquire** | Proof card: old rate struck in orange, new rate counts up (needs written sign-off) |
| 50–55s | Comment "calls" and I'll send you the 10-minute checklist, mate. | Which one is **yours**? | End card: question, orange button Comment "calls", locatedigital.io |

AU markers: Adelaide, mate, GA4 (neutral).
