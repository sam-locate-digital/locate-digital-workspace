## Hook
- **Title card:** I left corporate to build this
- **Spoken hook:** "If you don't know me yet, I'm Sam. I left my corporate job to build something I'd wanted for years."
- **Frame-1 visual:** Sam at the desk in a real, slightly messy work setting, direct eye contact, title card already on screen.
