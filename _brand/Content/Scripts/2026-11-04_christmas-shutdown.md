# Reel: The Christmas shutdown

**Format:** value list, ~50s
**Post by:** end of November, 7–9pm AEDT (it needs to land before people start winding down)

## Hook
- **Title card:** Why your January will be quiet
- **Spoken hook:** "Here's the one thing that decides whether January is dead for your business."
- **Frame-1 visual:** split screen. Top: two January calendar cards, one empty, one booked out. Bottom: Sam to camera.
- **Formula:** The one thing (and rule out the obvious). Inspo: [rooftop "one thing" reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/C8QFlapP9YW/)

## Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Here's the one thing that decides whether January is dead for your business. | Why your January will be **quiet** | Split screen: empty vs booked January calendars |
| 4–10s | And no, it's not your ad budget, your socials or the economy. It's what you do with the enquiries that land between now and Christmas. | Not your **budget** | Wide, then punch in on "now and Christmas" |
| 10–18s | One. Follow up every quote from the last 60 days before you close. One text: "Still keen to get this booked in for January?" | 1 Follow up every quote | Framework card builds step 1; phone screen with the text typed out |
| 18–26s | Two. Update your Google hours for the shutdown. If someone rings on the 29th and gets nothing, they go straight to the next business. | 2 Fix your Google **hours** | Screen recording: special hours in the Google Business Profile editor |
| 26–34s | Three. Swap "we're closed" for an auto-reply and voicemail that books them into January, with a link. | 3 Book them, don't **bounce** them | Phone screen: auto-reply text with a booking link |
| 34–42s | Four. Don't switch your ads off. Drop the budget, keep them running. Plenty of Aussies still search over the break, and they fill your first week back. | 4 Keep ads **running** | Framework card step 4; punch in |
| 42–47s | **[Proof]** [Sam: real example, e.g. a client who did this last year and how many January bookings came from the break] | [Sam: real number] **bookings** | Proof card, client name blurred (needs written sign-off) |
| 47–52s | Comment "January" and I'll send you the follow-up text and the auto-reply I use, word for word. | Is your January **booked**? | End card: question, orange button Comment "January", locatedigital.io |

AU markers: Christmas shutdown, "the 29th", Aussies, Google hours.

## CTA
- **Keyword:** January
- **They get:** the follow-up text message and the auto-reply/voicemail script
