## The prompt (for the screenshots, and to share in comments if asked)

```
You're helping our firm triage a new enquiry. Read the enquiry below and reply with:
1. Who it is: name, and how they contacted us.
2. What they need, in one sentence.
3. How soon they need us, and why you think so.
4. A short draft reply in a warm, plain Australian tone. Thank them, say what happens next, and ask the one question we'd need answered before a first call.
Don't promise anything about rates, products, fees or outcomes. If something is unclear, say so instead of guessing.

Enquiry:
[paste the enquiry here]
```
