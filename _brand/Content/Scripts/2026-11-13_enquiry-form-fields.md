# Reel: The 3 fields your enquiry form actually needs

**Format:** value list, ~48s, about 155 words
**Post:** Fri 13 Nov, 7–9pm AEDT
**Area:** marketing that brings enquiries

## Hook
- **Title card:** The 3 fields your form actually needs
- **Spoken hook:** "Here's the one thing on your website that decides whether people get in touch. And no, it's not the design, the photos or your Google ranking."
- **Frame-1 visual:** comparison split: a long 11-field form (dummy) next to a 3-field form.
- **Formula:** the one thing (rule out the obvious). Inspo: the rooftop "one thing" reel (https://www.instagram.com/p/C8QFlapP9YW/).

### Trial hooks (same body)
1. **Failed-fix question.** Title card: "Visitors but no enquiries?". Spoken: "Why is your website getting visitors but hardly any enquiries, even after the redesign?"
2. **Reframe.** Title card: "Ask for less". Spoken: "The fastest way to get more enquiries from your website is to ask for less."
