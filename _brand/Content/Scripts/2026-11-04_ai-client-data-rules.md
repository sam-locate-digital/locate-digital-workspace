# Reel: Four AI rules before you paste in client data

**Format:** value list, ~55s
**Post:** 7–9pm AEDT
**Before filming:** get the four rules checked. This is privacy and compliance territory, so the wording needs to be right.

## Hook
- **Title card:** 4 AI rules before you paste in client data
- **Spoken hook:** "If you want to use AI but don't want to leak client details, here are four rules."
- **Frame-1 visual:** split screen. Top: a chat window with a dummy client name and TFN pasted in, the TFN highlighted in blue. Bottom: Sam to camera. Dummy data only.
- **Formula:** Want, but don't want. Inspo: hook-library line "If you want to do this, first do this" ([inspo](https://www.instagram.com/p/C5AmorRpUqF/)) and [Mino Lee "un-slop" reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/De0yEPUN0RQ/)

## Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | If you want to use AI but don't want to leak client details, here are four rules. | 4 AI rules before you paste in client **data** | Split screen: chat window with a dummy TFN highlighted |
