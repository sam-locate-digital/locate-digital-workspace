# Reel: Your cheapest lead is already in your inbox

**Format:** quick hit, ~30s, about 95 words
**Post:** Sun 15 Nov, 12–1pm AEDT (quick and tactical)
**Area:** marketing that brings enquiries

## Hook
- **Title card:** Your cheapest lead is already in your inbox
- **Spoken hook:** "The cheapest lead you'll ever get is the one who already enquired and never heard back."
- **Frame-1 visual:** a phone showing an unread enquiry notification, dated two days ago (dummy).
- **Formula:** reframe. Inspo: Chris Williamson ft. Alex Hormozi "the fastest way to..." (https://www.instagram.com/p/De-Tn1fRH7g/).

### Trial hooks (same body)
1. **Failed-fix question.** Title card: "Reply before you buy more leads". Spoken: "Why are you paying for more leads when last week's enquiries still haven't had a reply?"
2. **The one thing.** Title card: "Who wins the client?". Spoken: "Here's the one thing that decides who wins the client. And no, it's not price, reviews or your website."

## Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | The cheapest lead you'll ever get is the one who already enquired and never heard back. | Already in your **inbox** | Phone close-up: unread enquiry from 2 days ago (dummy) |
| 4–9s | You've already paid for the ad, the website and the click. The only thing missing is a reply. | Missing: a **reply** | Wide |
| 9–16s | So here's the rule. Every enquiry gets a real reply the same business day. Not an auto-responder. A person, with a name. | Same business **day** | Punch in on "same business day" |
| 16–24s | Can't call yet? Send two lines. "Thanks Sarah, got your note about the ATO letter. I'll ring you tomorrow at ten. Does that suit?" | Two **lines** | Split screen: the two-line text on a phone (dummy) |
| 24–30s | Because the business that replies first usually gets the conversation. And the cheapest lead you'll ever get... | Reply **first** | Wide, cut on "get..." so it loops into frame one |

AU markers: ATO, "ring", "Does that suit?". Quick hit, so no proof beat or keyword CTA; the last line loops into the hook.

## CTA
- None (loop ending). Optional caption question.

## Caption
> The cheapest lead you'll ever get is the one who already enquired and never heard back. You've paid for the ad, the website and the click. The only thing missing is a reply. One rule fixes most of it: every enquiry gets a real reply from a real person the same business day, even if it's two lines. How long does an enquiry wait at your place before someone replies?
>
> #smallbusinessaustralia #ausbusiness #smallbusinessmarketing #leadgeneration
