**Area:** marketing that brings enquiries
**Format spec:** 5a Teaching carousel (built to be saved), from locate-content `references/formats.md`: 8 slides, 1080 × 1350 (4:5), canvas `#FBFCFF` with the faint `#E6E6E6` grid, white cards with a `#E6E6E6` border and 32 px radius, Public Sans 600–800, one emphasis word per slide in Newsreader italic with the blue brush underline, blue numerals in Newsreader 600 (1, 2, 3, never 01), Locate Blue `#007AE8` accents, orange `#FF5900` only on the last slide's button.

## Slide copy

1. **Cover**
   - Hook: 5 numbers your marketing report is **missing**
   - Subline: Clicks don't sign clients.
2. **Why it matters**
   - Most reports count clicks and impressions.
   - A principal wants to know one thing: did it bring in **clients**?
3. **1 Enquiries, by source**
   - How many people got in touch, and where from: Google, Meta, referrals, walk-ins.
4. **2 Replied the same day**
   - How many of those enquiries got a real reply the same business **day**.
5. **3 Meetings booked**
   - How many enquiries turned into a first meeting or call.
6. **4 Clients signed**
   - How many meetings became paying clients. Your CRM already has this, even if nobody's **looking**.
7. **5 Cost per meeting**
   - Total spend divided by meetings booked. The one number that tells you if the marketing is **working**.
8. **Recap and CTA**
   - Checklist (blue ticks): Enquiries by source / Replied same day / Meetings booked / Clients signed / Cost per meeting
   - Line: Ask for these five at your next marketing **meeting**.
   - Orange button: Save this
