Your marketing report should answer one question: did it bring in clients?

Clicks and impressions don't. These five numbers do: enquiries by source, how many got a reply the same day, meetings booked, clients signed and cost per meeting. If your report doesn't show them, ask for them. If nobody can pull them, that's worth knowing too.

Save this for your next marketing meeting. Which of the five could you find today?
