The short version

Six rules every edit follows

0.0sTalk from the first frameNo logo sting, no intro. The hook caption is on screen in frame one.
2–4sSomething changes every few secondsA cut, a punch-in or a graphic. Never more than 4 seconds on an unchanged frame.
1–3Words per captionWord-timed, centred at 62% height. One serif italic emphasis word with a blue brush every 5 to 8 seconds.
1Accent colourLocate Blue carries every graphic. Orange appears once, on the final call to action.
LightGraphics live on a light gridSoft white canvas, faint grid, white cards with real numbers. No dark tech panels.
−14LUFS, voice firstClean voice, quiet instrumental bed underneath, a soft click when a graphic lands.

1 · Fonts and captions

Plain sans for speech, serif italic for the word that matters

Two of the references pair a clean sans with a serif italic for emphasis. That is already Locate's type pairing, so captions read as ours without copying anyone.

Caption facePublic Sans 800, 64 px on a 1080 frame. White with a soft shadow on footage, ink #0D0D0D on the light canvas.
Setup linePublic Sans 600, 52 px, sits directly above an emphasis word.
Emphasis wordNewsreader 600 italic, 112 px. A blue brush (#007AE8) sweeps under it in 0.35 s. Use it once every 5 to 8 seconds, on the number, outcome or turn of the sentence.
Chunking1 to 3 words, at most 2 lines and 18 characters a line. Timed to the spoken word with Whisper.
PositionCentred, bottom of the text block at 62% of frame height (y ≈ 1190 px). This stays clear of the Instagram caption area.
Entrance0.14 s fade-in, scale from 92% to 100%. No bounce, no typewriter effect, no colour-per-word karaoke.
WritingSentence case, Australian spelling, numbers as digits ($2,850, 9 days). No emoji or exclamation marks in captions.

2 · Colour

One accent, the way every reference does it

Each reference picks a single accent and sticks to it: green, red or blue. Ours is Locate Blue. Footage keeps a natural, clean grade with true skin tones and no heavy LUT.

Locate Blue · #007AE8Brush underline, name tag, step numerals, chart lines, highlight marker.

Cyan · #75DFEESoft glow in a canvas corner and guide lines only. Never text.

Orange · #FF5900The end-card button and nothing else. Once per reel.

Canvas · #FBFCFF + #E6E6E6 gridBackground for every full-screen and split-screen graphic.

60% neutral and footage30% blue6% cyan4% orange

Same ratio as the brand guidelines. Where a reference uses a yellow highlighter on stats, we use a blue marker at 14% opacity.

4 · Zooms and transitions

Two framings, hard cuts, nothing flashy

Every reference uses plain hard cuts and moves between framings to keep a single talking head alive. None use spins, glitches or whip pans.

Wide100%, head and shoulders, eyes on the upper third line.
Punch118% from the same clip, cut in on emphasis lines. Alternate wide and punch on jump cuts so a cut never looks like a glitch.
Slow push100% to 105% across any take longer than 6 seconds.
SilencesCut every pause longer than 0.25 s. Keep breaths that sound natural.
TransitionsHard cut between shots. Graphics enter in 0.2 s, sliding up 40 px with a fade (ease-out). They leave on a cut.
B-rollReal screens, the client's real office, real documents with private details blurred. No generic stock money or handshake shots.

6 · Music and sound

The voice carries it

All five references are mixed to the same loudness and have music quietly under the speech. Sound effects can't be measured from a file, so that row is our choice, not something copied.

Loudness−14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than −1 dBTP. All five references measured between −14.1 and −14.6.
VoiceNoise removed, high-pass at 80 Hz, light compression, a little presence around 3 to 5 kHz. Recorded on a lapel or close mic, never the phone's built-in mic from a distance.
MusicInstrumental only: soft piano, warm lo-fi or minimal electronic, 85 to 110 BPM. It sits about 20 dB under the voice and ducks further while you speak. Licensed track or Instagram's library, one track per reel.
Sound effectsA soft click when a card lands, a low whoosh into a full-screen graphic, a small tick when a count-up stops. No more than one every 4 seconds, never on caption words, never a cash register or airhorn.
EndingMusic lifts slightly under the end card and finishes on the last frame, so the loop back to the start feels clean.

9 · Never in a Locate reel

Things that make it look like everyone else

  • Tracked uppercase captions, or Inter, Poppins and Montserrat
  • Emoji, exclamation marks or hype words in captions
  • Rainbow or colour-per-word karaoke captions
  • More than one orange element in a reel
  • Spin, glitch, zoom-blur or whip-pan transitions
  • Stock footage of cash, handshakes or people pointing at laptops
  • Speeding the voice past 1.1×
  • Logo intros or "hey guys" openings before the hook
  • Numbered markers written 01, 02, 03
  • Real client names, figures or documents on screen without written sign-off
