How we collect personal information

We may collect personal information either directly from you, or from third parties, including where you:

  1. (a)contact us through our website;
  2. (b)receive services from us;
  3. (c)submit an enquiry, sign-up or advertising lead form;
  4. (d)communicate with us in person or via email, telephone, SMS, social applications (such as LinkedIn or Facebook) or otherwise; and
  5. (e)interact with our website, social applications, services, content and advertising.

For example, our clients may give us access to customer or subscriber information, or we may collect enquiry details through campaigns we manage for them.

We may also collect personal information from you when you use or access our website or our social media pages. This may be done through use of web analytics tools, ‘cookies’ or other similar tracking technologies that allow us to track and analyse your website usage. Cookies are small files that store information on your computer, mobile phone or other device and enable and allow the creator of the cookie to identify when you visit different websites. If you do not wish information to be stored as a cookie, you can disable cookies in your web browser

We use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and remarketing tools to measure website and campaign performance and show relevant advertising, including on other websites and social media. These tools may disclose device, browsing and advertising interaction data to Google and Meta, which may combine it with other information they hold. You can manage personalised advertising through the providers’ advertising settings. Further information is available in Google’s privacy policy and Meta’s privacy policy.
