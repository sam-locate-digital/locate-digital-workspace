Pricing & plans

What it costs and what's included.

What does it cost? +

Plans start at $1,800 a month plus your ad spend. Most businesses start on our Growth plan. On the free audit we'll recommend what fits your goals and budget — no hard sell.

What's the difference between the management fee and ad spend? +

Two separate things:

  • Management fee — what you pay us to build, run and optimise your campaigns and reporting.
  • Ad spend — what Google and Meta charge to actually show your ads. That's billed directly to your own accounts, so you keep full visibility and control of it.

We never mark up your ad spend. Every dollar of it goes to the platforms.

How much should I budget for ad spend? +

It depends on your services, your catchment and how many new customers you can handle. As a rule of thumb, we suggest enough spend to gather meaningful data quickly rather than trickling it out. We'll recommend a starting figure in your free audit and adjust as the results come in — you're never locked to a number.

Is there a setup fee? +

The campaign and tracking build happens in your first month as part of getting started — we'll confirm exactly what's included when we scope your plan on the audit call.

Getting started

From first call to live campaigns.

What's the free audit, exactly? +

A short call where we look at your current ads and Google listing and point out what's leaking enquiries and what we'd do differently. You keep the notes either way. No obligation to work with us.

How do we get started? +

Three steps:

  • Free audit — we review what's running now and show you the gaps.
  • Build — in month one we set up your tracking and campaigns, targeted to your catchment and best services.
  • Grow — we run and optimise every month and report on real bookings.

How soon will campaigns be live? +

Usually within the first two weeks, once we've got access to your accounts and tracking is in place.

Do I need a website or landing pages already? +

A working website helps, but you don't need a perfect one. Where it makes a difference, we build service-specific landing pages matched to what people are actually searching for, so your ad clicks land somewhere built to convert.

Working together

Contracts, ownership and communication.

Am I locked into a contract? +

No. We ask for the first 90 days so the campaigns have time to work, then it's month to month. You stay because it's working, not because you signed something.

Who owns the ad accounts and data? +

You do. We set up and run campaigns inside your own Google and Meta accounts, so if we ever part ways, your accounts, history and data stay with you. No holding your assets hostage.

How often will we be in touch? +

You'll get a plain-English report every month covering bookings, cost per booking and what we're doing next, plus we're reachable between reports whenever something comes up. We'll agree a rhythm that suits you when you come on board.

Can I pause or change my plan? +

Yes. After the initial 90 days you can scale up, dial back or pause — just give us reasonable notice so we can wind campaigns down cleanly rather than leaving spend running.

Results & reporting

How we track it and what you'll see.

How soon will I see results? +

Campaigns are usually live within the first two weeks. Google Ads can bring enquiries quickly. Meta and the optimisation work build over the first one to two months as the data comes in.

How do you track actual bookings, not just clicks? +

We wire up GA4 and conversion tracking to your real bookings and enquiries, so every dollar of spend is tied to an outcome you care about — not vanity metrics like impressions and reach.

What does the reporting look like? +

One monthly report in plain English: new customers booked, cost per booking, and what we're doing next. The numbers that matter, none of the jargon.

What if it's not working? +

You'll know first — because you can see the real numbers. If something's underperforming we adjust it, and if a channel isn't earning its place we'll tell you straight. There's no lock-in keeping you somewhere that isn't delivering.

Services & fit

Whether we're the right match for you.

Do you work with my type of business? +

If you're a Melbourne service business that takes bookings or enquiries — physio, osteo, dental, allied health, legal, trades — most likely yes. The free audit is the quickest way to find out.

Do you only do Google and Meta ads? +

They're our focus because, for local service businesses, that's where the booking-ready demand is. We also handle the tracking, conversion setup and landing pages that make those ads actually pay off. If a different channel genuinely fits your goals, we'll say so.

Do you work with businesses outside Melbourne? +

Melbourne service businesses are our specialty, which is why we understand local catchments so well. If you're elsewhere, get in touch — we'll be upfront about whether we're the right fit.

What makes you different from a generalist agency? +

We specialise in service businesses, so we're not learning your world on your budget. You get results you can see, tracking tied to real bookings, local targeting so you're not paying for clicks from the other side of the city, and no lock-in contract.
