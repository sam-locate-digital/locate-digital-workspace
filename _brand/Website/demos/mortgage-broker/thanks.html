What to have ready

Not needed for the first call, but they speed things up afterwards.

  • Your last two payslips, or two years of tax returns if you're self-employed
  • Photo ID, like a driver licence or passport
  • Three months of bank statements
  • Your current home loan statement, if you have one
  • Details of other loans and credit card limits
  • A rough monthly budget
