How we help

Banks often say no to the self-employed because the application didn't show the full picture. We make sure it does.

  • Lenders that read business income properly

    Some add back things like depreciation or one-off costs. We know who does.

  • Options with a short trading history

    Some lenders accept less than two years of tax returns, depending on your work history.

  • Low-doc loans, if they fit

    Where full financials aren't ready, we explain the alternatives and what they cost.

  • Working with your accountant

    We ask for the right documents the first time, so there's no back and forth.

Documents to have handy

You don't need all of these for the first chat. They help us move quickly once you're ready to apply.

  • Personal and business tax returns for the last two years
  • Notices of assessment from the ATO
  • Recent business activity statements (BAS)
  • Business and personal bank statements
  • Your ABN and GST registration details
  • Your accountant's contact details

Self-employed questions

What sole traders and business owners ask us most.

How long do I need to have been trading?

Many lenders like two years, but some accept less. It depends on your industry and what you did before you went out on your own.

What is a low-doc loan?

A loan that uses other evidence, like BAS or an accountant's letter, instead of full tax returns. They can cost more, so we compare them with standard options first.

My last year was lower. What then?

Some lenders use your most recent year, others average two. We'll match you with a lender whose approach suits your figures.

Can I apply if I'm a contractor?

Yes. Some lenders treat contractors more like employees, using your current contract rate. We'll find the ones that do.
