What to do while you wait

Burst pipe

  1. Turn off the water at the main tap, usually beside the meter at the front of the property.
  2. Turn off your hot water system.
  3. If water is near power points or lights, switch the power off at the switchboard, but only if you can reach it while staying dry.
  4. Move valuables and towel up what you can.

Gas smell

  1. Don't use light switches, phones or flames near the smell.
  2. Open doors and windows.
  3. Turn off the gas at the meter if it's safe to reach.
  4. Get everyone outside, then call us. If the smell is strong, call 000 first.

Power fault

  1. Check the switchboard for a safety switch or breaker that has tripped.
  2. Unplug the appliances on that circuit, then switch it back on.
  3. If it trips again, leave it off and call us.
  4. Stay well away from damaged, sparking or burning wiring.
