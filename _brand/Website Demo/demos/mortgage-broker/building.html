Building a home, financed in stages

A construction loan pays your builder in stages as the work gets done. We'll help you set it up, check the contract timing and keep the payments moving.

What we'll handle

Building has more moving parts than buying. We keep the finance side simple.

  • Land and build, together or apart

    Buying land first, a house and land package, or a knockdown rebuild, we structure it to match.

  • Progress payments

    The lender pays your builder at each stage. We explain what happens and when.

  • Contract and plans the lender will accept

    Lenders usually want a fixed-price contract and approved plans. We check the paperwork early.

  • Any first home help

    Building a new home can open up grants or concessions. We'll check what you're eligible for.

How progress payments work

A typical build is paid out in stages like these. Your builder's contract sets the exact stages and amounts.

  1. Stage 1Base

    The slab or footings are down. The lender inspects or checks the builder's invoice, then pays.

  2. Stage 2Frame

    The frame is up. You start paying interest on what's been drawn so far.

  3. Stage 3Lock-up

    Roof, windows and external doors are in.

  4. Stage 4Fixing

    Kitchen, bathrooms and internal fit-out.

  5. Stage 5Completion

    Final inspection and handover. Your loan switches to normal repayments.

Good to know before you sign

A few things that catch people out, so they don't catch you.

  • Variations need the lender's okay

    Changes to the plans can change the loan. Tell us before you sign a variation.

  • Plan for rent and a loan at once

    If you're renting while you build, you may be paying both for a while. We'll factor that in.

  • Keep a buffer

    Lenders usually ask you to cover cost overruns yourself before they pay more out.

Building questions

What people ask us before they start a build.

Do I pay interest on the full loan while building?

Usually not. Interest is charged on the amount paid out so far, and many lenders let you pay interest only during the build.

What if the build runs over budget?

Lenders usually need you to cover extra costs yourself before more is paid out. We'll talk about a buffer early so it isn't a surprise.

Can I build as an owner-builder?

It's harder and fewer lenders allow it. We'll tell you honestly whether it's an option for your project.

Do you help with knockdown rebuilds?

Yes. These are common in Melbourne. We'll plan for demolition, the build and where you'll live in the meantime.
