Questions we hear a lot

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask us on a free chat.

What does a mortgage broker cost?

Usually nothing. Lenders pay us a commission when your loan settles, and we'll show you how much before you choose. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm fee policy]

How much can I borrow?

It depends on your income, expenses, debts, deposit and each lender's rules. We'll give you a rough figure in your free chat, then check it properly against real lender rules.

Will talking to you affect my credit score?

No. A first chat and estimate don't involve a credit check.

What deposit do I need?

Often 5 to 20% of the price, depending on the lender and any scheme you qualify for. We'll check what applies to you.

When should I refinance?

When a fixed rate is ending, your rate hasn't been reviewed in a while, or you want to access equity. We'll tell you if it isn't worth it.

How long does pre-approval take?

Often a few days to two weeks, depending on the lender and your paperwork.

Do you work with self-employed people?

Yes, including sole traders and new businesses. See how we help self-employed borrowers.
