Self-employed questions

What sole traders and business owners ask us most.

How long do I need to have been trading?

Many lenders like two years, but some accept less. It depends on your industry and what you did before you went out on your own.

What is a low-doc loan?

A loan that uses other evidence, like BAS or an accountant's letter, instead of full tax returns. They can cost more, so we compare them with standard options first.

My last year was lower. What then?

Some lenders use your most recent year, others average two. We'll match you with a lender whose approach suits your figures.

Can I apply if I'm a contractor?

Yes. Some lenders treat contractors more like employees, using your current contract rate. We'll find the ones that do.
