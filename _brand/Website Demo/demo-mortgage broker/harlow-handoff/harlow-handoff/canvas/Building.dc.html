How progress payments work

A typical build is paid out in stages like these. Your builder's contract sets the exact stages and amounts.

  1. Stage 1Base

    The slab or footings are down. The lender inspects or checks the builder's invoice, then pays.

  2. Stage 2Frame

    The frame is up. You start paying interest on what's been drawn so far.

  3. Stage 3Lock-up

    Roof, windows and external doors are in.

  4. Stage 4Fixing

    Kitchen, bathrooms and internal fit-out.

  5. Stage 5Completion

    Final inspection and handover. Your loan switches to normal repayments.

  • Plan for rent and a loan at once

Building questions

What people ask us before they start a build.

Do I pay interest on the full loan while building?

Usually not. Interest is charged on the amount paid out so far, and many lenders let you pay interest only during the build.

What if the build runs over budget?

Lenders usually need you to cover extra costs yourself before more is paid out. We'll talk about a buffer early so it isn't a surprise.

Can I build as an owner-builder?

It's harder and fewer lenders allow it. We'll tell you honestly whether it's an option for your project.

Do you help with knockdown rebuilds?

Yes. These are common in Melbourne. We'll plan for demolition, the build and where you'll live in the meantime.
