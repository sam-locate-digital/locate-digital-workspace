First home questions

The things people ask us most before their first purchase.

How much deposit do I need?

Often 5 to 20% of the price. With a smaller deposit you may pay lenders mortgage insurance, unless a scheme or a family guarantee applies. We'll check your options.

Can I get pre-approval before I find a place?

Yes, and we recommend it. Pre-approval shows what you can spend and helps you move quickly. It isn't a final approval, so the lender still checks the property later.

What other costs should I plan for?

Stamp duty (unless you're exempt), conveyancing, building and pest inspections, lender fees and moving costs. We'll give you a list for your situation.

Can my parents help?

Yes. A family guarantee uses some of their home's equity as extra security. It's a big decision for them, so we explain the risks to everyone before anyone signs.

What happens on auction day?

We make sure your pre-approval is current and you know your limit. If you win, you'll usually pay the deposit on the day, so we plan for that early.
