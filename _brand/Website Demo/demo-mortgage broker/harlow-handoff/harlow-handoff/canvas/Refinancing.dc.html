Refinancing questions

Short answers to what people ask before they switch.

How often should I review my loan?

At least once a year, and whenever a fixed rate is ending or your situation changes. Our clients get a free review every year.

Does refinancing cost anything?

There can be discharge fees, government registration fees and, for fixed loans, break costs. We add them all up before you decide.

Can I refinance to renovate or pay off other debts?

Often, yes. We'll check your equity and whether rolling debts into your home loan makes sense, since spreading them over a longer term can mean paying more interest overall.

Will refinancing affect my credit score?

A new application adds an enquiry to your credit file. That's why we only apply once we've found a loan that's worth switching to.
