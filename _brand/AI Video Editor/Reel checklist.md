# Reel checklist: faster edits, faster renders

Tick these off and a reel goes from raw clips to finished export in one pass, with no back-and-forth. Each item notes the time it saves, based on the first reel (2026-11-04).

## 1. Before you film

- [ ] **Have a script.** Write it with `/reels-script` (or `/locate-content` for the week) and save it in `Scripts/YYYY-MM-DD_topic.md`. With a script I line the captions up with your exact words: no guessing "gonna need" vs "not going to", or "Claude" vs "code". *Saves the biggest chunk of time.*
- [ ] **Know your brand mentions and numbers.** Every product you'll name (GoHighLevel, Claude, Canva…) and every figure you'll say ($200,000, 3 months).
- [ ] **Have proof ready.** Screenshots of the results you'll mention, cropped to the numbers that matter.
- [ ] **Batch it.** Film several scripts in one sitting, then I edit them back to back.

## 2. Camera settings (iPhone)

- [ ] **1080p at 30 fps** (Settings → Camera → Record Video). The 4K clips took over 10 minutes just to convert. Only use 4K if you want extra-sharp zooms. *Saves about 10 min per reel.*
- [ ] **Vertical, eye level, phone steady** (tripod or a stack of books).
- [ ] **Leave headroom.** Your eyes about a third of the way down, with space above your head. Captions and graphics then sit clear of your face, and I don't have to re-position anything.
- [ ] **Clip-on mic** (you have one). Quiet room, phone on airplane mode.
- [ ] **Light in front of you**, not behind.

## 3. While filming

- [ ] **One continuous recording if you can**, rather than lots of short clips. Seven clips meant seven files to convert, transcribe and sort.
- [ ] **Flubbed a line? Stop, stay silent for 2 full seconds, then redo the whole sentence** from its first word. The clear gap tells me where the retake starts. Half-restarts ("Here's an automation that… here's an automation that") hide inside the audio and were the slowest thing to find and fix.
- [ ] **The last take of a line is the keeper.** Don't go back to an earlier line after moving on.
- [ ] **Say brand names clearly and fully**: "GoHighLevel", "Claude".
- [ ] **Start talking straight away** and end on your last word. No "hey guys", no "okay, so".

## 4. Hand-over

Put everything for the reel in one folder: `Raw Footage/YYYY-MM-DD_topic/`

- [ ] The clip or clips
- [ ] Any screenshots, named in the order they appear (`1.png`, `2.png`…)
- [ ] A `notes.md` filled in like this:

```
Script: Scripts/2026-11-05_topic.md
Brands mentioned: GoHighLevel, Claude
Screenshots: 1.png = 3-month automation, 2.png = 6-month, 3.png = 12-month
Numbers to show: $200,000+ this year
Call to action: "The prompt is in the comments"
Pause cut: 0.1 s (or leave as the 0.25 s default)
Anything to skip or keep: e.g. "use the second take of the hook"
Client sign-off for figures on screen: yes / no
```

- [ ] Then just say: **"Edit the reel in Raw Footage/<folder>."**

## 5. Reviewing

- [ ] **Skip the storyboard** for routine reels, since the style is locked. Ask for one only for something new.
- [ ] **Approve a quick preview first.** Say "preview first" and I render at half size in about 2 minutes, instead of about 9 for the full-quality export.
- [ ] **Watch it once and send all your changes in one message, with timestamps** ("0:05 repeated line, remove the end card…"). One round instead of three.
- [ ] **Small tweaks yourself:** wording, music or trimming can be done in CapCut with the CapCut pack, without a re-render.

## 6. What I do on my side to keep renders quick

- Work on C: (not Google Drive) and render 2 frames at a time, the fastest setting that doesn't crash on this computer.
- Reuse the saved kit (`remotion-kit/`): captions, glass canvas, cards, 3D icons, logo tiles, music and mix are already built.
- Check every cut automatically for leftover pauses and hidden repeated lines before you see it.
- Only render the full-quality version after you approve the preview.
