# Reel: Ring your own business

**Format:** quick hit, ~30s
**Post:** 12–1pm AEDT (quick and tactical; people can actually try it at lunch)

## Hook
- **Title card:** Call your own business today
- **Spoken hook:** "The best marketing you'll pay for this year is someone answering your phone."
- **Frame-1 visual:** a mobile on a desk ringing, unanswered, with the ring sound under Sam's first line.
- **Formula:** Reframe. Inspo: [Chris Williamson "fastest way" reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/De-Tn1fRH7g/) and hook-library line "Why does nobody talk about the cost of (insert action)" ([inspo](https://www.instagram.com/p/DIbM_TgS5se/))

## Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | The best marketing you'll pay for this year is someone answering your phone. | Call your own business **today** | Close-up: phone ringing on a desk, nobody picks up |
| 4–9s | Because every ad, every review, every post ends in one place. Your phone ringing. | Every ad ends **here** | Sam to camera, punch in on "one place" |
| 9–18s | So try this, mate. Ring your own number at 12:30 on a Tuesday, when everyone's at lunch. Count the rings. Listen to the voicemail. Then do it again at 5:15 on a Friday. | Ring at **12:30** | Sam dialling on a mobile; cut to a phone screen showing the call timer |
| 18–25s | Whether you're a tradie, a physio or an accountant, ask yourself: would I book here? Or would I ring the next one on Google? | Would you **book**? | Screen recording: a Google map search with three listings, Sam's thumb moving to the second one |
| 25–30s | Because they've got two more numbers to try. And they're already dialling. | They're already **dialling** | Back to the ringing phone from frame 1 (loops to the start) |

About 95 words (around 25–30s at your pace). AU markers: mate, tradie, physio, Google map search. No keyword CTA: it's a quick hit built for sends and the loop.

## Caption
> The best marketing you'll pay for this year is someone answering your phone. Ring your own business at 12:30 on a Tuesday and see what a new customer hears. How many rings did you get to?
>
> #smallbusinessaustralia #aussiebusiness #smallbusinessmarketing #customerservice

## Shoot notes
Two phones: one on the desk to film ringing out, one in Sam's hand to dial. Grab a real Google map search screen recording for a common service in your suburb, with business names blurred. End on the same ringing-phone shot as frame 1 so the loop is clean.
