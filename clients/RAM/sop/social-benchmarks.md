# How the big players run retail-property social — and what we take

Quick benchmark of how major retail landlords (Westfield/Scentre, Mirvac, Vicinity) and smaller neighbourhood centres run their social, and how it shapes the RAM precinct approach. The takeaway: **the big malls win on budget and scale; neighbourhood centres win on being genuinely local.** We lean into local.

## What Westfield / Scentre Group do

- **Every centre has its own local marketing.** Centre-specific channels and newsletters, run by a dedicated team — not one national account. Validates our "each precinct as its own brand" decision.
- **Retailer spotlights + cross-promotion.** They actively feature individual retailers and run partner promotions. Retailers reshare → free reach.
- **An annual "what's on" calendar** built on seasonal and occasion hooks: Back to School, Lunar New Year, Easter, Mother's/Father's Day, EOFY and toy sales, Halloween, Christmas. This calendar is the backbone of the content year.
- **Experience-led, "Living Centre" positioning** — the centre as a destination and community hub, not just shops.

## What smaller / neighbourhood centres do well

- **Community-first content.** The centre as a local meeting place that fights isolation — people, not just product. This is the neighbourhood centre's edge over a giant Westfield.
- **Instagrammable / experiential moments.** Photo walls, seasonal portrait setups (e.g. Christmas pet photos) that give shoppers a reason to visit *and* post.
- **User-generated content via a branded hashtag** (e.g. a centre's own #tag) so the centre can repost shopper photos — cheap, authentic content.
- **Exclusive community feel** — workshops, local partnerships, things shared first/only on Instagram.
- **Seasonal alignment** — social calendar joined up with sales periods.

## What we take into the RAM system

These fold into the 8 pillars and the SOP. The five that matter most for our 7 neighbourhood precincts:

1. **Lead with local community, not corporate.** Our differentiator vs the big malls. People, regulars, local moments. (Pillar 6.)
2. **Retailer cross-promotion is free reach.** Tag the retailer in every spotlight; they reshare to their audience. Build the habit. (Pillars 1, 2, 7.)
3. **Run a branded UGC hashtag per centre.** Repost shopper content (with permission). Set one per precinct in its brief. Low-cost, authentic.
4. **The seasonal calendar is the backbone.** Map Back to School → Lunar New Year → Easter → Mother's/Father's Day → EOFY → Halloween → Christmas into every precinct's calendar first, then fill around it. (Pillar 5.)
5. **Create one Instagrammable moment per centre per season.** A photo wall or seasonal setup = content + footfall + UGC in one. Pitch these to RAM as low-cost activations.

## What we deliberately don't copy

- **No investor/corporate content** at the precinct level (unit price, fund news) — that's REP's channel.
- **No big-budget influencer/paid productions** to start — neighbourhood authenticity beats polish here.
