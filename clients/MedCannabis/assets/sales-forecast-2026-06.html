Victoria accounts for roughly 25% of Australia's population and a similar share of medicinal cannabis patients. That's approximately 55,000–60,000 Victorian patients with an active or recent prescription. Melbourne alone — roughly 70% of Victoria's population — represents a local addressable market of around 40,000 current patients, growing at 13% a year.

A new clinic doesn't need to serve all of them. It needs to serve a few hundred well. At 250 active patients, average monthly revenue per patient of $160 (consultations plus dispensing margin), this clinic generates $40,000 a month. That's a realistic 12-month target in the base case.

What moves the numbers

Key growth drivers

High impact

GP referral relationships

Each active GP referrer sends 2–5 patients a month. Five GPs referring consistently means 10–25 new patients/month from one channel. This is the single highest-leverage activity in year one.

High impact

Google Business Profile

Local "near me" and suburb-specific searches drive walk-in and same-day bookings. A fully optimised GMB profile with 25+ reviews is worth roughly 8–12 organic enquiries a month with zero ongoing spend.

High impact

Patient retention

Chronic condition patients don't leave unless the service disappoints them. Every patient retained is worth $126/month indefinitely. Retention from month one determines whether year 2 starts at $280k ARR or $100k ARR.

Medium impact

External script dispensing

Patients arriving with existing prescriptions from GPs generate dispensing margin without a consultation cost. This is essentially pure margin revenue once the patient is acquired through any channel.

Medium impact

SEO and organic search

Takes 6–12 months to generate meaningful volume but is cumulative. A page ranking for "medicinal cannabis clinic Melbourne" is worth 15–30 organic enquiries a month at zero marginal cost once built.

Medium impact

Review velocity

Google reviews directly influence how often the GMB listing appears in local results. A clinic with 50+ 4.8-star reviews ranks significantly higher than one with 10. Set up a post-appointment review request from day one.

Looking ahead

What year 2 looks like

If the base case plays out, the clinic enters year 2 with 185 active patients generating $23,310/month in recurring dispensing and consultation revenue. That's a $280k ARR base before any new patient acquisition in year 2.

Year 2 projections — base case, assuming continued growth at a similar pace: 400–450 active patients by month 24. Monthly revenue: $50,000–$57,000. Annual year 2 revenue: approximately $640,000–$750,000.

At this point the clinic is a genuine business. Year 3 questions become: second location, expanded telehealth nationally, or both. The referral reputation built in years 1–2 is the asset that makes those decisions possible.

Note: These projections are planning models, not guarantees. They rest on assumptions about patient acquisition rates, retention, and revenue per patient that will need to be calibrated once real data is available. Update the model monthly once the clinic is live. The assumptions here are based on market averages and Locate Digital's experience with similar healthcare businesses.
