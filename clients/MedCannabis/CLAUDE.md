## ⚠️ Compliance — critical, read before every piece of work

Medicinal cannabis in Australia is one of the most tightly regulated advertising environments. Every piece of content, copy, and marketing produced for this client must comply with the following.

### TGA and Therapeutic Goods Act

- Medicinal cannabis products containing THC are Schedule 8 (controlled drug) under the TGA
- CBD-only products may be Schedule 4 or lower — confirm product schedule before writing about specific products
- **Section 42DL of the Therapeutic Goods Act prohibits advertising prescription-only medicines (Schedule 4 and Schedule 8) directly to the general public**
- This means: no promotional copy for specific cannabis products in any consumer-facing channel

### What this means in practice

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Educational content about conditions (chronic pain, anxiety, etc.) | Advertising or promoting specific cannabis products |
| Information about how medicinal cannabis works generally | Claiming a product treats, cures, or prevents a condition |
| "Talk to a doctor" / "access through a prescription" framing | Direct calls to action to buy or order specific products |
| Clinic/telehealth services (prescriptions, consultations) | Before/after testimonials about product effectiveness |
| GMB: clinic info, hours, services listed | Posting product promotions or discounts on GMB |
| Email to existing patients (opted in, consented) | Unsolicited email with product promotions |
| Brand awareness content that doesn't reference specific products | Any paid ads on Google or Meta for cannabis products |

### Paid advertising platforms

- **Google Ads**: Policy prohibits advertising cannabis, even medicinal. Do not recommend Google Ads for product campaigns.
- **Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)**: Prohibits cannabis advertising. Limited brand-only campaigns may be possible — check policy before recommending.
- **Alternative paid channels**: Programmatic display via cannabis-friendly DSPs (e.g., Mantis, Traffic Roots) may be available — flag to client if relevant.

### Content guidelines

- All blog and social content must be educational, not promotional
- Always include: "Medicinal cannabis is a prescription medicine. Speak to a doctor to find out if it's right for you."
- Do not mention specific product names, brands, or formulations in public-facing content
- Do not make therapeutic claims that imply guaranteed outcomes
- No dosage guidance in public content
- If creating condition-specific landing pages for SEO: stick to condition information, patient pathways, and clinic services — not product details

### Social media

- Instagram and Facebook posts must follow Meta's community standards on regulated goods
- No product imagery that shows cannabis in a recreational light
- Educational infographics and patient-pathway content are the safest approach
- Do not run paid promotions on posts that mention medicinal cannabis
