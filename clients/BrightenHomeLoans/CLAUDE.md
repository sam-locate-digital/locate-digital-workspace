## Target audience
Australians who need flexibility, speed, or criteria that the Big 4 banks won't accommodate:

- **Refinancers** — existing homeowners 35–55 looking to reduce repayments, access equity, or escape a big-bank rate. Often motivated by a fixed rate expiry or a life change. Want a fast, low-friction switch.
- **Construction borrowers** — buyers building a new home or investors developing property. Need progressive drawdown, flexible approval timelines, and a lender who understands the build process. Age 30–50.
- **Bridging loan borrowers** — homeowners buying before their current property sells. Time-pressured, needs certainty of funding quickly. Age 35–60.
- **Common thread** — creditworthy borrowers who want speed, transparency, and a lender that treats them as an adult. Not first home buyers or high-risk borrowers.

## Compliance & restrictions

**ASIC — National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009**
Brighten holds an Australian Credit Licence (ACL — number TBD). All advertising must comply:
- Any rate advertised must be accompanied by the **comparison rate** (e.g. "5.89% p.a. comparison rate 6.12% p.a.")
- Comparison rate disclaimer required: *"Comparison rate based on a $150,000 loan over 25 years. WARNING: This comparison rate applies only to the example given..."*
- No misleading claims about approval certainty ("guaranteed approval", "always approved")
- No implied specific financial advice — general information only
- Responsible lending obligations apply — ads must not encourage borrowing beyond means

**Design and Distribution Obligations (DDO) — effective Oct 2021**
Each product must have a Target Market Determination (TMD). Copy must not actively market outside the TMD. Confirm product TMDs with Brighten's compliance team before writing product-specific ad copy.

**Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles)**
Financial data is sensitive personal information. Reporting must use aggregated/anonymised data only. No identifiable customer data in dashboards, reports, or any file outside Brighten's systems. API integrations must comply with Brighten's privacy policy — confirm before connecting any data source.

**Credit advertising specific rules**
- ACL number must appear in all credit advertisements
- "Credit provided by Brighten Home Loans Pty Ltd. Australian Credit Licence [number]" in ads
- Interest rate claims require comparison rate disclosure
