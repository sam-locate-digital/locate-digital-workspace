# Brighten Home Loans — Welcome sequence
Date: 2026-05-26
Platform: TBD
Goal: Drive direct loan applications — convert enquiry into completed application
Audience segment: New direct enquiries (Google Ads / Meta Ads — not broker-referred)

---

## Campaign overview

Four emails across 10 days. The goal is to take someone who just enquired and build enough trust and clarity that they complete an application. Brighten's core advantage — speed, flexibility, and eligibility criteria that the Big 4 won't touch — needs to come through in every email. Tone is professional and competent. No jargon without a plain-language explanation next to it.

## Email 3 — Your situation
**Send:** Day 5–7
**Audience:** All new direct enquiries (can be segmented by loan type if platform allows)

**Subject lines:**
- A: A few things worth knowing before your loan assessment
- B: Before we speak — useful context on your options
- C: Fixed rate expiring soon? Read this before your call.

**Preview text:** Understanding the basics saves time on the call and gets you a faster answer.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

Before we connect, a few things that are worth knowing.

**If you're refinancing:**
The most common reason people come to Brighten is a fixed rate ending — often the new variable rate from their current lender is significantly higher. We can assess whether switching makes sense, what the break costs might be, and whether you'd come out ahead. It usually takes less than a week to get a clear answer.

**If you're building:**
Construction loans work differently to standard home loans — funds are drawn down progressively as each stage is completed. A good lender will have dealt with builders and valuers hundreds of times and won't hold up your project waiting for paperwork. We have.

**If you need bridging finance:**
Buying before you've sold. It's time-pressured and the stakes are high. Bridging loans have a specific structure — we'll walk you through it clearly so you know exactly what you're committing to.

Whatever your situation, we'll give you a straight answer on what's possible. No vague commitments.

Speak soon.
