# Gloss Hair Studio — Welcome sequence
Date: 2026-05-26
Platform: Mailchimp (assumed)
Goal: Convert first-time booking into a retained regular client
Audience segment: New clients who have booked or visited for the first time

---

## Campaign overview

*Test client — fictional. Context used: mid-market hair salon, South Yarra Melbourne, target audience women 25–45, services include cuts, colour, balayage, treatments. Tone: warm, stylish, not corporate. Goal: rebook.*

Four emails across three weeks. A new client is the most expensive client to acquire — the goal of this sequence is to make sure they come back. Warm tone, personal feel, light on imagery in body copy. Rebooking CTA in every email from Email 3 onward.

---

## Email 1 — Welcome
**Send:** Within a few hours of first visit (or on booking confirmation)
**Audience:** All new clients

**Subject lines:**
- A: Great to meet you — thanks for coming in
- B: Welcome to Gloss, [First name]
- C: Your first visit — a note from us

**Preview text:** We hope you loved it. Here's a little something to say thanks.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

Thanks for coming in to Gloss.

We hope you loved your [colour / cut / treatment] — and that [Stylist name] took good care of you.

A couple of things from us:

**Keep it looking great:** [Stylist name] recommended [product name] for your hair type. You can grab it at the salon or ask us to set one aside next time you're in.

**Your next appointment:** Most clients find [6–8 weeks] is the sweet spot for a refresh. If you'd like to lock in your next visit now, you can book online any time.

[Book your next appointment → LINK]

See you soon,
The Gloss team

*Gloss Hair Studio · South Yarra · glosshairstudio.com.au*

## Email 2 — Meet the team
**Send:** Day 3–4
**Audience:** All new clients

**Subject lines:**
- A: A bit more about us (and who does what)
- B: Behind the chair at Gloss
- C: The team you'll be seeing

**Preview text:** Every stylist at Gloss specialises. Here's how to find your match.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

We're a small team at Gloss — which means you'll get to know us pretty quickly.

Each stylist specialises in something different. [Stylist 1] is our colour specialist — balayage, blonding, and lived-in colour. [Stylist 2] focuses on precision cuts and smoothing treatments. [Stylist 3] is brilliant with curly and textured hair.

If you'd like to try a different stylist next time, or if your first visit was with whoever had availability, just mention it when you book — we'll match you with the right person.

We're in South Yarra Tuesday to Saturday. Easy parking on [Street name], or a short walk from [nearest station].

[Book your next appointment → LINK]

The Gloss team

*Gloss Hair Studio · South Yarra · glosshairstudio.com.au*

---

## Email 3 — Soft rebook
**Send:** Day 14
**Audience:** New clients who haven't rebooked

**Subject lines:**
- A: About time for a refresh?
- B: [First name], your hair is probably ready
- C: 6 weeks goes fast — book before we fill up

**Preview text:** Saturday mornings are filling up. Lock in your next visit now.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

It's been a couple of weeks since your visit — if you had a colour, it's around the time things start to shift a little.

We're booking out [2–3 weeks] ahead at the moment, so if you want your preferred time and stylist, it's worth locking in now.

[Book your next appointment → LINK]

See you soon,
The Gloss team

*Gloss Hair Studio · South Yarra · glosshairstudio.com.au*

---

## Email 4 — Review ask + loyalty
**Send:** Day 21
**Audience:** New clients who haven't rebooked or left a review

**Subject lines:**
- A: One small favour — and a little something for you
- B: How was your first visit?
- C: Leave us a review — takes 60 seconds

**Preview text:** Your feedback helps other people find us. And we'd love to hear from you.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

Quick one.

If you enjoyed your visit to Gloss, would you mind leaving us a Google review? It genuinely helps new clients find us and takes about a minute.

[Leave a Google review → LINK]

And if anything wasn't quite right, just reply to this email. We'd rather know.

Thanks for coming in — we hope to see you again soon.

The Gloss team

*Gloss Hair Studio · South Yarra · glosshairstudio.com.au*
