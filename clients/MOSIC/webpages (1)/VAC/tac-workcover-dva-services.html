TAC, WorkCover & DVA services

Recovering from an injury can be challenging. At Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre, we provide osteopathic treatment for patients with approved Transport Accident Commission (TAC), WorkCover, and Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) claims.

Whether your injury occurred on the road, at work, or during your service, our experienced osteopaths are here to support your recovery. Through hands-on treatment, exercise rehabilitation and personalised advice, we aim to reduce pain, restore movement, and improve function.

From your first appointment through to your recovery, we'll assist you through the claims process and work closely with members of your healthcare team to ensure your care is coordinated every step of the way. Wherever you are in your recovery, our team can give you the confidence and advice to achieve the best possible outcome.

Every claim type has its own eligibility requirements and referral process. Choose a service below to learn more about how osteopathy can support your recovery and what you need before your first appointment.
