## Caption

Ran Melbourne a few weeks ago and now stairs are the hardest part of your day?

That's often runner's knee. The joint behind the kneecap gets irritated after a big training block, and going downstairs, downhill or into a squat loads it the most.

The sore spot isn't always the cause. Stiff ankles, weak hips and a jump in kilometres often play a part, which is why we look at the whole chain, not just the knee.

For the first fortnight, ease off rather than stop, leave hills and deep squats out for now, and build back with short, flat runs. If it's not settling after two weeks, it's worth getting looked at properly.
