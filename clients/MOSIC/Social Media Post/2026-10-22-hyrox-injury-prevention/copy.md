## Caption

Seven weeks until Melbourne Hyrox race week, and this is when the niggles start to show up.

Running volume and station work tend to ramp up at the same time. Calves, shins and Achilles usually feel the running first. Lunges and wall balls on tired legs load the front of the knee, and the sled finds your lower back once form starts to fade.

Most of these aren't bad luck. They're load and fatigue, and they're usually easier to manage when they're caught early.

This week: practise the stations in race order, and keep your run kilometres climbing slowly.

Niggle sharper than last week, or still there the next morning? Worth getting it looked at before race week.

Book online at melbourneosteopathycentre.com.au or call (03) 9663 6202. No referral needed. Bourke St CBD and Essendon.
