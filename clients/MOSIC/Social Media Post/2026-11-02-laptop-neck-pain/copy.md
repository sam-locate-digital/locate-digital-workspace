## Caption
Feel it right across the back of your neck by mid-afternoon? Check where your laptop is sitting.

When the screen sits low, your head drifts forward and the muscles at the back of your neck and shoulders hold it there for hours. It usually builds slowly, until one afternoon it just aches.

Two fixes that cost nothing:
1. Raise the screen so the top of it is level with your eyes. A stand or a stack of books works, with a separate keyboard and mouse.
2. Every 30 minutes, get up. Refill your water, walk to the window, roll the shoulders.

If it's still nagging after a few weeks, or it's bringing on headaches, it's worth getting looked at properly.
