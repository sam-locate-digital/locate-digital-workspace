Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre
January + February 2026

Executive summary

Traffic is up, but conversion is the bottleneck. Total website visitors grew +13.4% YoY and organic search +5.6% YoY — both healthy signals. The booking funnel is leaking after the click: confirmed Cliniko bookings dropped −8.9% YoY, Google Ads phone calls −38.1% YoY, and total new patients −73.0% YoY.

Diagnosis: people are reaching the site but the landing pages aren't closing the deal. The fix is already in motion — search campaigns are being rebuilt and landing-page copy revised this period. Q1 marketing calendar is complete; four new articles shipped; a HotDoc account was opened (15 bookings already booked at Essendon).

Watch next month: phone-call recovery from the rebuilt Google Ads campaigns, and whether the new landing-page copy lifts the click-to-booking rate.
