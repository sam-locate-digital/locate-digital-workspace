Melbourne Winter

Cold ground works against you

Muscles are less compliant in the cold, and grounds are either rock hard or churned to mud. Neither is kind to ankles and knees.

Straight from a desk in the CBD to a pitch in Docklands or Essendon? Give yourself longer to warm up than the ten minutes in the car.

5/7
Before Something Goes

5 things that cut your risk

01

Sprint before you have to sprint

Build-up runs at 70, 80, 90% during the week

02

Nordic curls, twice a week

Two sets. Strong evidence for cutting strains

03

Warm up for real

10 minutes minimum, not just static stretches

04

Strengthen glutes and adductors

Copenhagen planks, bridges, split squats

05

Respect the load

Add one session at a time, not two at once
