Pain between the shoulder blades 💻

November is crunch time in a lot of offices. Longer days at the desk often show up as a deep ache between the shoulder blades, usually on one side and worse by mid-afternoon.

What's usually behind it
• Slumped sitting: as the upper back rounds, the muscles between the shoulder blades are held on a stretch for hours.
• Head drifting forward: the closer you lean to the screen, the harder the upper back works to hold your head up.
• Mouse arm: reaching for a mouse or trackpad all day loads one side more than the other.
• Stress and shallow breathing: tension sits in the shoulders, and the rib joints beside the spine can stiffen.

Who tends to get it
• People working long hours on a laptop with no separate screen
• Anyone whose day goes desk, car, couch, with little movement in between
• People adding heavy pulling or pressing at the gym on top of a stiff upper back

Signs it's building
• A burning or knotted spot beside the spine
• A sharp catch when you take a deep breath or turn to look behind you
• Stiffness that eases when you move, then returns after a meeting

Get checked promptly if the pain comes with chest pain, shortness of breath, pain spreading down the arm, or if it doesn't change with movement or position.

Picking this up early usually makes it easier to settle.
