# MOSIC — Reactivation campaign (lapse)
Date: 2026-05-26
Platform: Cliniqapps (integrated with Cliniko)
Goal: Drive bookings from patients who haven't visited in 3–6+ months
Audience segment: Active patients with no appointment in the past 90–180 days

---

## Campaign overview

Three emails across two weeks. MOSIC has a 12,000-patient base — a small percentage of lapsed patients rebooking represents significant monthly revenue. Tone is warm and human, written as if it's coming from the clinic directly. No guilt. No hard sell. The patient should feel genuinely noticed, not marketed to.

AHPRA compliance applied throughout — no outcome claims, no "fixed", "cured", or clinical result guarantees.

---

## Email 1 — Check-in
**Send:** Day 0
**Audience:** Patients with no booking in 90–180 days

**Subject lines:**
- A: It's been a while — how are you going?
- B: Checking in from MOSIC
- C: [First name], we haven't seen you in a while

**Preview text:** No pressure — just wanted to see how things are tracking.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

It's been a few months since your last visit and we just wanted to check in.

How's the [shoulder / back / neck] been? (We know life gets busy and appointments slip.)

If things have been manageable, that's great. But if something's been nagging or you've been meaning to get back in, now's a good time. Our practitioners at [Bourke St / Essendon] have availability coming up.
