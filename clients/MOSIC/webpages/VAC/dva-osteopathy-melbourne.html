Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) osteopathy

At Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre, we're proud to support veterans and members of the Australian Defence Force community. We welcome Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Gold Card and eligible White Card holders at both our Melbourne CBD (Bourke Street) and Essendon clinics.

Eligible DVA osteopathy appointments are bulk billed directly to the Department of Veterans' Affairs, meaning there are no out-of-pocket costs for approved treatment. To access bulk-billed osteopathic treatment through DVA, you'll need:

  • Your DVA Gold Card or eligible White Card
  • Your DVA client number
  • A current referral from your GP

If you don't yet have a GP referral, please contact our friendly administration team. We'll be happy to discuss your circumstances and help guide you through the next steps.

If you have questions about your DVA eligibility or entitlements, you can also contact the Department of Veterans' Affairs directly.

What to expect from your treatment

Our experienced osteopaths provide personalised treatment designed to reduce pain, improve movement and function, and support your overall health and wellbeing.

Every treatment plan is tailored to your individual needs and goals. Whether you're managing a longstanding condition or recovering from a more recent injury, our practitioners will work with you to help you stay active, independent and doing the things that matter most to you.
