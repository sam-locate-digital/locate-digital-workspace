Transport Accident Commission (TAC) osteopathy

At Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre, we're here to support your recovery following a transport accident. Our experienced osteopaths provide personalised care to help reduce pain, restore movement and function, and support your return to work, sport and everyday activities.

If you've been injured in a motor vehicle accident, you may be eligible to claim reimbursement for osteopathic treatment through the Transport Accident Commission (TAC).

How to access TAC osteopathy

Accessing treatment through the TAC is straightforward:

  1. Report your transport accident to the TAC and lodge a claim.
  2. Wait for your claim to be accepted and receive your TAC claim number.
  3. Contact our friendly team to book your appointment and provide your TAC claim details.

If you have sustained a serious injury following a transport accident, you should seek immediate medical attention before arranging osteopathic care. For less serious injuries, we recommend seeing your GP and lodging your TAC claim as soon as possible.

If you haven't yet lodged a claim, you can find more information about eligibility, benefits and the claims process on the TAC website.

What to expect from your treatment

Every treatment plan is tailored to your individual injury, recovery goals and stage of healing. Our osteopaths use evidence-informed treatment and rehabilitation strategies to help you recover safely and confidently.

Treatment may help to:

  • Reduce pain and stiffness
  • Improve movement and physical function
  • Support your recovery after injury
  • Help you return to work, sport and everyday activities

Treatment costs

Once your TAC claim has been approved, eligible treatment costs can be claimed through the TAC.

Please note that the TAC may not reimburse the full cost of ongoing osteopathic treatment, and a gap payment may apply depending on your claim and treatment requirements. Our administration team will always let you know about any out-of-pocket costs before your appointment, so there are no unexpected surprises.
