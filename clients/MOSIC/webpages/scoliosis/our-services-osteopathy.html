What is Osteopathy?

Osteopathy is a holistic system of diagnosis and treatment, emphasising the body's structural integrity. Like a finely tuned engine, a balanced body functions with minimal wear and tear, conserving energy for everyday activities.

Benefits of Osteopathy

Melbourne Osteopathy Sports Injury Centre offers a comprehensive approach to injury management:

  • Holistic treatment tailored to individual needs
  • Accurate diagnosis using advanced orthopaedic tests
  • Identification of the root cause, not just symptom relief
  • A broad range of techniques: soft tissue therapy, manipulation, and functional movement

Functional Movement & Osteopathy

Functional Movement principles are another key element of Osteopathy and are based on the premise that how we move very much affects the structures of the body. When treating the body as a whole chain, an Osteopath can quickly recognise deficits in one area that are detrimentally affecting others. You can read more about Functional Movement here.

Techniques Used by Our Osteopaths

Our experienced osteopaths use a combination of techniques for effective treatment:

  • Mobilisation: Soft tissue massage, joint movements, and muscle stretches
  • Manipulation (Thrust Technique): Restores joint function with quick, precise movements
  • Soft Tissue Therapy: Rhythmic stretching and deep pressure to alleviate tight muscles
  • Muscle Energy Techniques: Activates muscle contraction to restore symmetry and movement
