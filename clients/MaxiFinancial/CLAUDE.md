## Compliance & restrictions
**Mortgage broking — NCCP Act 2009 / ASIC.** Maxi Financial operates under an
Australian Credit Licence (ACL) or as a credit representative. Advertising rules:
- Best Interests Duty applies — do not imply outcomes that aren't in the borrower's interest
- No misleading or deceptive comparison rate / interest rate claims; any rate quoted must
  include the comparison rate and required warnings
- No unqualified "best", "lowest rate", "guaranteed approval" claims
- Credit licence / credit representative number must appear where required (site footer, ads)
- No promise of loan approval or specific borrowing capacity without qualification
- "Subject to lender approval, terms and conditions apply" where relevant

**Privacy Act (APPs):** no identifiable borrower/financial data in reports — aggregated
and anonymised only.
