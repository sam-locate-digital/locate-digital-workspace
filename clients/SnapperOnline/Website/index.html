Andre Kong  ·  Photographing weddings since 2010

A note from Andre

I'm a Sydney wedding photographer. I've been at this for fifteen years and a few hundred weddings, and the best ones still feel the same: nobody's posing, everyone's a bit tired, the light is doing something soft to a face I haven't met before today.

I work quietly. I don't move people around. I don't ask for the moment again. I just turn up, pay attention, and bring back a set of pictures that look exactly like the day you remember, even the parts you didn't notice were happening.
