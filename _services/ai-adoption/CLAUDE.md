# AI Adoption — new Locate Digital service (in development)

Internal initiative, not a client. This folder holds research and material for a potential new service line: **AI training/upskilling for businesses**. Nothing here is client work — it's Locate Digital evaluating and shaping its own offer.

## What it is

Training and upskilling only — workshops/courses that teach a business's staff to use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.) inside their existing workflows. Explicitly **not** in scope for this version of the offer: systems implementation, workflow automation builds, or AI agent development. If research surfaces strong demand for implementation work, that's a separate future offer to evaluate on its own.

## Status

Pre-launch. Market research complete; positioning and pricing built out for the first vertical (see below). Launch itself is decided — remaining work is finishing the go-to-market material and deciding the open items listed at the bottom of this file.

## Target customer

Decided, first vertical: **mortgage brokers and finance professionals**. Market research (`assets/market-research-2026-09.*`) covered the broader small-business market; the finance/broking vertical was picked afterward as the first beachhead because of a strong, well-sourced angle (74% of advice practices use AI, only 39% have a governing policy — NCCP/Best Interest Duty can't be delegated to a tool). Other segments (trades, healthcare) were also flagged as underserved in the original research and remain candidates for a second vertical later.

## Go-to-market material (built)

- `pitches/mortgage-brokers-finance-pitch.html` + `.pptx` — sales-call slide deck (source of truth for offer content: problem framing, NCCP/Best Interest Duty risk, 4-step process, pricing tiers $997 / $2,200 / $4,500+, why-us)
- `pitches/ai-training-brokers.html` — web landing page built from that deck, with its own `pitches/assets/` (logo, hero video, photography)

The offer, once and for all: a **half-day workshop** on three named pain points (document checking, marketing, admin) + a **written NCCP-aware AI use policy** + **90-day check-ins**. Training/upskilling only — not a live AI product, not systems implementation, not agent development. Any new material for this service (more pages, ad copy, proposals) should pull positioning and numbers from the pitch deck / landing page, not be invented fresh.

## Geography

Australia-wide.

## Research brief

- **Purpose:** shape positioning and pricing for launch (not validating whether to launch)
- **Competitor scope:** include a light scan of existing players — AI training providers, RTOs, consultants, and software vendors offering onboarding/enablement — enough to show whitespace, not a full competitor-research-depth teardown
- **Positioning angle:** none preset. Do not assume "we use AI ourselves" or a vendor-partner/certification angle as the hook — let the research surface what's differentiated or underserved instead
- **Depth:** full market-landscape pass (size/growth, trends, regulatory, audience segments, demand signals, pricing benchmarks) per the `market-research` skill
