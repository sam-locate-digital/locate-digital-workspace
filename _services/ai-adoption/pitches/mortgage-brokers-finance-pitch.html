The problem

Three jobs are eating the one you're actually good at.

You didn't get into broking or advice to be a document checker, a content creator, and a data-entry clerk. But that's most of the week now.

  • Checking documents by eye, file after file. Payslips, bank statements, ID, serviceability inputs — manually cross-checked on every single application, the same repetitive checks a tool could flag in seconds.
  • Marketing that keeps getting bumped. Social posts, EDMs to your referral network, follow-up content — the first thing dropped when the pipeline gets busy, so it never happens consistently.
  • Admin that swallows 15–20 hours a week. Around 70% of brokers spend 3+ hours a day on processing and file work, and compliance is the second-biggest driver of it, behind only loan reviews.
