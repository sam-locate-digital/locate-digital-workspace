The problem

Three jobs are eating the one you're actually good at

You didn't get into broking or advice to be a document checker, a content creator and a data-entry clerk. Most weeks, that's exactly where the time goes.

  • Checking documents by eye, file after file. Payslips, bank statements, ID, serviceability inputs — manually cross-checked on every application, the same repetitive checks a tool could flag in seconds.
  • Marketing that keeps getting bumped. Social posts, EDMs, follow-up content — the first thing dropped when the pipeline gets busy.
  • Admin that swallows 15 to 20 hours a week. About 70% of brokers spend three-plus hours a day on processing and file work.
The real risk

Staff are already using AI. Is anyone checking how?

Adoption has outrun governance across the finance sector — and under the NCCP Act, that gap sits with you, not the software vendor.
